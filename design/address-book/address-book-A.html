A · Sections — the iteration

Two questions answered: where the connect door goes (and what happens to the +), and how an address moves between groups. Everything else from A is unchanged.

1 · The way in — one field, one door, no plus

The + is deleted. It could only ever duplicate the field, and it can't be the connect door either: connecting has a waiting state ("Waiting — tap to cancel") and can sprout a manual-pairing card underneath it, which a nav-bar glyph has nowhere to put. So: the field is the manual way in, the row beneath it is the automatic one. Two doors, two shapes, and the row collapses away the moment you start typing.

2 · The field does both jobs

One field, and what you typed decides. Words → the book filters and everything above it folds away. An address, ENS or .sol → the verbs arm, the preview arrives, and the connect row steps aside. This is already how the model works — book.search(draft) and addressPreview read the same string today.

3 · Moving an address between groups

Three doors, in order of how often they're used. A group is a label on entries in the model, and an address may carry several — so the sheet ticks rather than picks, and "moving" is one untick plus one tick. Nothing new is stored.

4 · The whole screen

Decisions this makes — say if any is wrong

  • The + is deleted. Two doors instead: the field (type/paste) and the row under it (connect a wallet app). Neither duplicates the other.
  • The connect row collapses while you type. It is only relevant on an empty field, so it steps aside for the preview rather than stacking under it.
  • The WalletConnect mark needs bundling as an imageset (we have no brand-walletconnect today) — that is the only new asset in the whole redesign. Variant ii ships with no new art.
  • Groups become a strip of cards, not sections in the list (this reverses §433's folder sections). Tapping a card opens the group on its own screen. That is what keeps the list from starting three screens down, and it gives a group somewhere to be — including a drop target.
  • The list default becomes A–Z, not Recent. §433 made it recent-with-watched-first because a bulk paste of forty could push your watched wallets off the bottom — the shelf at the top fixes that outright, and A–Z with sticky letters is how you find one of twenty-seven. Recent and Most active stay in the sort menu.
  • The sky comes out. The spine replaces it. Say if you want it kept as a collapsed card instead.
  • Nothing new is stored — no Thing field, no CloudKit deploy, no new network read. The group model, the watch cap, the star, the read-only promise and the Connection door are all untouched.
