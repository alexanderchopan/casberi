The address book, redesigned

Three directions for the wallet manager. Everything here obeys the standing rulings: no balances anywhere on this screen (§435), no hairlines, sentence-case headers, one gesture per row. Faces are the real seeded identicons from WalletFace.swift, so what you see is what the app draws.

All three answer the same four asks: watched (capped at 5), saved (N), groups, move an address between groups — and each shows connections only across the five you watch, which is the one set the corpus can actually prove relationships for.
