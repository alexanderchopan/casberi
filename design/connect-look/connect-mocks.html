Two pages, one job — and the form that every design pass flowed around

48 of 56 offers need setup. For every one of them, Connect on the product page doesn't connect — it pushes a second page, where the same icon, the same tagline and the same preview greet you again above a field.

The form-y look has a date. §186 (2026-07-23) deliberately froze the pre-connect form — "it's important to know what the steps are" — and every pass since (§189 slabs, §190's one-sentence rule, §202's roster) flowed around it. Result: ~20 screens still wear Settings furniture — gray section labels above every control, gray paragraphs below, numbered steps set in 17pt body. TokenSetupScreen alone carries 5 section headers and 5 footers.

What changed, precisely

  • Step 1 becomes the button it was describing. Every keyed bridge's step 1 is literally "Open <url>" — as text it's a chore you retype; as a filled slab it's the action. Steps go 3 → 2 without deleting a word of instruction.
  • "Get your token" / "Token" labels go. The placeholder says what to type, the verb says what happens — §190's exact ruling.
  • Five footers become one sentence. The long version already lives on the product page you arrived from.
  • The repeated mark, tagline and preview drop. You read them on the way in.
§186 is respected: the steps are still there, still visible, still numbered, nothing behind a disclosure. Only the furniture around them left.

Recommendation — A + C

A removes the read-before-you-act gate with the least invention: the sheet component already exists and already carries this exact form, so the "form" the user liked survives untouched — it just arrives when you ask for it instead of standing between you and the app.

C is what actually fixes "form-y", and it's the bigger win of the two. Step 1 becoming a real button is worth doing on its own merits: right now ~20 screens tell you to open a URL and then refuse to open it.

B is the purist answer and it's right for the keyless half of the catalog — but on a keyed bridge it opens the page on homework, and it re-litigates §186. If you want B, the honest version is B for keyless, A for keyed: the page grows a field when the field is one line, and raises a sheet when there are steps.
