1 · Feed rows: one anatomy

Before, each source drew its own row. The lead sat at 26, 36 or 44pt, pictures went left or right, and a bundle's count took the corner where every other row put the time. After, every row is the same four slots on its dark plate. The pieces vary, but their places never do.

3 · Chips: two kinds, verbs become rows

A pill now means one of two things: a choice you tap, or a fact you read. Anything that does something, like Connect, Copy or Open, is a row. In the catalogue the whole row already did the verb, so the pill was the same action drawn twice.
