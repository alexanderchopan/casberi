C · One row style

One anatomy on every row: lead, name and time, content, picture, one quiet line. Every lead is 26pt, a source mark or a person's face, so the text column has one left edge from top to bottom. A band: source, time, one line, pictures only as 44pt tiles under the name. A post: person, network and time, the words, then media or a quote, then one quiet count.

How dark can a card be

Frame C's rows again, each on a plate that costs no height: the row's own 10pt padding becomes the plate's, and the plate bleeds 12pt past the text column so the marks keep the day header's edge. Left to right the fill lifts 2%, 3% and 5% off pure black. The middle one is the app's own darkest surface token; the right one is the last step before it reads as gray.

F · #0d0d10 (5%)

The last step before gray. It reads as a card everywhere, and the black is now a dark charcoal.

What each shape holds

A band is a source telling you one thing happened. A post is a person speaking. Nothing else gets a shape of its own; the sheet is where a thing's full anatomy lives.

SlotBandPost
LeadSource mark, 26pt, Mark.rowPerson's face, 26pt, Face.row. Same edge as the mark.
Line 1Source name, 17pt · time, 13pt rightPerson, 17pt · network and time, 13pt right
Line 2One line, 15pt secondary: the title, the fact, the count. One line, always.The words, 17pt, up to six lines (whole in an archive room, §396a)
PictureZero to three 44pt tiles under the name (§719, §730). A thumbnail, an album, a screenshot and a token mark are all a tile.Media at full width under the words, or a quote block. Same 10pt radius as today.
TrailingNothing. The count moves into line 2, the sparkline leaves the row.One quiet 12pt count line.

Where the twenty go

TodayBecomesWhat it gives up in the row
BandRow, BundleRow, StripRowBandNothing. The count block on the right becomes line 2.
MediaRowBandThe right-hand thumbnail; it is a tile under the name. Duration joins line 2.
MusicRowBandThe art at the left edge; it is a tile. The mark leads, as on every row.
TokenRowBandThe sparkline and the two-line price block. Price and move are one sentence; the chart is the sheet's and the crown's.
ReadingRow, ExcerptRowBandThe excerpt. The headline is line 2, the excerpt is the sheet's first paragraph.
AppReviewRowBandThe star row on its own line; stars open line 2.
GitHub rowsBandThe tag chips under the title (§699). The type is a word in line 2 or a tag in the sheet.
PostCard, SocialThreadCard, ReplyingToRowPostNothing. The name takes the band's 17pt, and the network moves next to the time.
