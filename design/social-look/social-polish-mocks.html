The room reads 80 characters of every post

Two real findings from the code, before any taste is involved. One: PostCard renders thing.title — which is titleLine()'s hard 80-character clamp — while postText, the full post captured since §81, sits on the model unused. What I'd build

Ordered by payoff per unit of risk. The first three are corrections, not additions — the record already holds what they show.
1 · The post, in full

PostCard reads postText instead of the 80-char title, clamped to ~6 lines with a "more" affordance. The single biggest change to how the room reads.

One field swap + a line clamp.
2 · The card says why it's here

SocialThread.contextLabel — already written, already rendering in the All feed — moves into the card as a tinted pill: /design, Liked, Mentions you.

Reuses an existing function verbatim.
3 · Quotes and replies render in the room

SocialQuoteCard exists and renders in the sheet; the room shows nothing. A quote-post is currently a contextless line — the exact bug §81 fixed once already, still live one surface over.

Existing component, new call site.
4 · The network wears its own color

brandHue already knows Farcaster is #855dcd and Bluesky #0285ff. The room takes the §219 media wash treatment so the two rooms stop being interchangeable grey.

Existing modifier, two sources.
5 · A mention is a moment

Someone talking to you is the highest-signal event in social and it lands silently. Fires SourceMoments — the same bus that already rains for wallet highs and quiet returns.

One fire() call at the mention path.
6 · The crossing

Two watched accounts in conversation. Detected offline from a landed post's parent.ref/quote.ref resolving to another watched handle — zero network reads. Nothing else on the phone can see this.

New detector; pure corpus join.
7 · The pack landing

Following 34 people at once returns a silent number today. Rain in the network's hue, name the pack, let the roster fill in.

One fire() at the import.
8 · The person room's rhythm

A posting-cadence bar row at the head of the room I just built — drawn from capturedAt, the same read FeedHeatmap already performs for GitHub.

Existing read, new head.

Two things I'd deliberately not do. No engagement counts on the card — §219 ruled "numbers are annoyances," and a stored count is a snapshot of a moving number that would sit there going stale (the honesty rule's own example). And no unread badges or "N new since" tallies: the roster's ring already says who's new without counting at you.
