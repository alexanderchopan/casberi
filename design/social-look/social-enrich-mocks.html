What each one costs

1 · Link posts land the link

New: the embed's URL becomes a real .link thing, joined to the post as its why. Reuses Safari's reading anatomy — no new row type.

5 · The roster head

New shared component, but it's AssetRosterShelf under different data — round marks (people), same shelf the wallet and tokens already use.

6 · Threads collapse

Consecutive same-author replies group at render time — no ingest change, no new field, a feed-side fold over what's already landed.

7 · Every image shows

One-line fix: PostCard reads imageURLs instead of previewImageURL alone. The data has been captured since 2026-07-16.

2 · The person room

The biggest of the six — a new pushed screen replacing the 560pt tray, querying a person's things plus their verified-address transactions in one fetch.

4 · Starter packs

New keyless read (app.bsky.graph.getStarterPack) — unmeasured, needs a probe against the live AppView before it's built on.

Sequencing: 7 and 1 are the cheapest — land them first, they change what's already on screen with no new surface. 6 is a pure feed-side fold. 5 needs the roster wired to watched-people data (face + ring). 2 is the real project — a new screen, a new joined query. 4 waits on a measuring probe, same discipline as every other keyless endpoint in this codebase.
