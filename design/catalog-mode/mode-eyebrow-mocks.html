What this does and doesn't fix

  • Fixes: the person who opens Instagram, X, Snapchat or TikTok first now reads how it connects before tapping Connect, in the app's own six-case vocabulary rather than a paragraph they'd have to remember from onboarding.
  • Doesn't fix: the catalog grid. Row 1 still shows name + tagline only, and “Your posts and saves, findable” still reads like a live feed. If that's the surface that matters, the fix there is the four taglines, not a badge — you already ruled a badge on every row is wallpaper.
  • Doesn't touch: the connect page. Its chip, intro sentence and §315 prose budget are unchanged; this is the same fact stated one step earlier, not a second copy of it.
  • Every option costs one edit in AppDetailScreen.header. Option C additionally needs BridgeSetupMode lifted from the setup screens onto Offer — worth doing on its own merits (it would let setup-copy-audit.py assert every connectable offer declares a mode), but it's a bigger change than the eyebrow.
