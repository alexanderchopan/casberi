Applied to both cards: the Sankey dies into a split bar plus fat avatar rows; the range bar demotes to an outcome pill, appearing only where something is wrong.

Flow, reduced · a bar, a sentence, a list

The Sankey dies. One split bar carries the whole picture — in vs out, to scale, green vs ink — and a one-word sentence states the outcome ("Kept +$1.0K", which is the only thing Cash App would put there). Below it, the counterparties as fat avatar rows, biggest first, signed amounts bold: exactly the lanes the band computed, worn as the list this app already knows how to draw. Every row is a real landed thing, so taps come free.

What's gained: instant read, no geometry to squint at, half the height, and the rows inherit every existing behavior (icons, naming, sheets). What's given up: the ribbon picture of sources converging into you — the one thing v2 does that this can't. My lean: this one. The model (`WalletFlow`) is unchanged either way; only the view simplifies.

Liquidity, reduced · outcome pill, mechanism on demand

"Is it working" becomes a word. Every row states its outcome as a pill — Earning +$59 in the money green, Idle 3d in attention — because that's the question the card exists to answer, and a range bar makes the reader derive it. The fees join the pill, so the earning number finally wears the earning color.

The range bar survives only where it explains something: an idle row keeps a micro version (your range cold, the price dot outside it — WHY it's idle, at a glance), an earning row drops it entirely — in-range-ness is what "Earning" means, and drawing it twice is the tally instinct wearing geometry. Rows shrink to activity-list height; the card halves.

Law check: color is state and money only (green = earning/arriving, attention = idle, ink = spending — deliberate spending still isn't an alarm, per the "Owed" ruling); the split bar's two widths are magnitude; sentence case; no hairlines (the micro-range's ring is the card showing through); no dead controls — flow rows open their things, liquidity rows stay terminal reads as today. The "Other" row keeps the no-silent-caps rule: the folded tail is named and counted, never dropped. Nothing here animates.
