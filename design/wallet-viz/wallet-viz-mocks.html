1 · The flow band · "where did it go?"

The question the sparkline can't answer. The line says the total moved; this says through whom. Money moving is the doctrine's one blessed exception, and every ribbon here is already a landed thing (EVM transfers, Solana activity, Peer fills, Gnosis Pay spends) — the band is an arrangement of the corpus, not a new read.

In wears confirm, out wears ink. Arriving money is the app's one green moment (SourceMoments already rains for it). Outflow is deliberately not red — spending you did on purpose isn't an alarm; that's the composition strip's own "Owed" ruling extended. Slots as its own card after the balance card, replacing nothing.

5 · The slope board · grounded in first-seen

The Hyperliquid rule as a chart: never fabricate history you didn't observe. Each watch already stamps its own moment and price (the TokenWatch thing); the dumbbell draws exactly the two facts the app holds. The same primitive serves Kalshi/Polymarket positions — odds at save → odds now.

Why not sparklines? Per-token curves exist one tap away (TokenChartView). The board's job is comparison at the moment that's personal — since you cared — which no exchange chart shows. Slots at the head of the watchlist section. Slope angle is normalized per row (the % pill carries magnitude); a flat row draws gray and signless, per the isFlat rule.

What each rides on

Flow bandZero new reads. Landed transfer/fill/spend things + address book names. The work is grouping a window of the corpus by counterparty and drawing it.
Attribution barZero new reads. ValueSample's per-token snapshots — the mover line's own arithmetic, all terms kept. Verifies with -seedWalletHistory.
Risk stripZero new reads. WalletDeFi + MorphoDeFi + HyperliquidDeFi books, already fetched for the lending card. Needs one normalization function (HF → distance, %-to-mark → distance) — pure, harness-testable.
HorizonZero new reads. Query: wallet-sourced things with dueAt in the next 30 days. Verifies with -aerodromeProbe / -hyperliquidProbe landing real dueAt rows.
Slope boardNeeds one additive field: price-at-watch on the TokenWatch thing (stamped at watch time, backfilled nil — old watches honestly show "since Jul 31" from first sync). The only one touching Thing; purely additive, no migration.
Blast radiusReuses the prepare path's live allowance read (currently fired per-sheet, would fire per-render of the card — worth a §216-style window). Holdings factor is already computed for spam filtering.
SplitsZero new reads. Holdings + a static stablecoin symbol set + the chain each holding already carries.

Law check: no hairlines — the horizon's track and the blast-radius bars are recessed wells, the attribution zero-spine is a mark, not a rule; sentence case throughout; glass stays floating; color is identity-by-label, magnitude-by-tint-opacity, state-by-the-three-semantics wearing words (in = confirm because arriving money is the app's green moment; out = ink because deliberate spending isn't an alarm — the "Owed" precedent; approval exposure = attention, never destructive). Every hero declines its slot with nil under its floor, and every dot/ribbon/bar is a door to a real thing or a read — no dead controls.

Doctrine check: flow band and attribution are money moving (the blessed exception); the horizon is WHAT'S NEXT; risk strip, blast radius and splits are visualizations of held state; the slope board draws only observed moments (first-seen grounding, the Hyperliquid rule) — two points, never a fabricated curve. Nothing here is a tally wearing a chart.

Build order if approved (cheapest → dearest): splits → horizon → attribution → slope board → risk strip → blast radius → flow band. Each is its own commit with its own headless probe (the -roomInsightProbe lesson: the failure mode is a card silently owning another's slot, so each new card's decline conditions get logged, not just its happy path).
