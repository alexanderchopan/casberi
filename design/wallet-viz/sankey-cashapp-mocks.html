Same flow, chunkier convictions

A · Pipes · every lane its own object

The most Cash App drawing move: each lane is one fat rounded stroke — solid green in, solid slab out, no gradients, round caps — with the counterparty's disc sitting in its mouth like a Cash App avatar row bent into a flow. The biggest pipe is thick enough to carry its label INSIDE in the dark-on-green money style; thin pipes label alongside.

Trade-off: pipes don't visually sum the way stacked ribbons do — you read lanes, not sides. The in/out totals in the header carry that instead.

B · The bleed band · color mass, square shoulders

The precedent pick. When the treemap went through this exact exercise, the ruling was: the crown is a parade of rounded capsules, so the standout move is breaking the shape grammar — bleed it edge to edge, square shoulders, a stripe of color mass. Applied to the Sankey: solid slabs run INTO the card's edges, connectors are straight tapers (no bezier softness), and the money type sits dark-on-green exactly like a Cash App balance pill.

Trade-off: the boldest and least "chart-like" of the three — it reads as a poster of your week. Slab opacity steps by rank, which spends opacity on hierarchy; the law reads that as magnitude restated, defensible but worth saying aloud.

C · The coin · everything meets you

The most playful: the wallet is a coin — your face, ringed — and every lane is a fat round-capped ribbon meeting its rim. Labels stop pretending to be axis text: each is a Cash-App-style pill riding its own ribbon, signed money bold, green pills in, slab pills out. The convergence-on-YOU picture the user wanted from the Sankey is at its literal strongest here.

Trade-off: pills need room; past ~4 lanes a side they'd start negotiating for space, and the fold ("Other") does more work. Thickness still carries magnitude, but the pill sizes don't, and two bold encodings sit close together.

Law check, all three: green = arriving (the app's green moment), out = ink slabs — never red (the "Owed" ruling); thickness/height = magnitude; the wallet's identity rides the spine or coin (face + tint); sentence case; no hairlines (rings are the surface showing through; B's connectors are fills, not rules); the folded tail is always named. Static, no motion. My lean: B — it's the treatment this app already ruled "the most Cash App move" once (the treemap band), and it makes the card the one object on the screen that isn't another rounded capsule. A is the safest; C is the most delightful and the most crowded.
