Flow band v2 · ports, not slabs

The inversion: bars shrink to 8pt ports at the card's edges — magnitude survives as segment height — and the ribbons take the whole middle. Labels move ONTO the ribbons (name + value in one line, icon optional), which kills v1's cramped in-slab captions and makes both sides the same species at last.

The spine is you now: the wallet's own face sits on it, and scoped to one wallet the spine wears that wallet's identity tint — "a wallet is a color in this app," finally applied to the card about your money moving. On All it stays the app tint.

Delight, two moments: on mount the ribbons draw on left-to-right, staggered — the sparkline's own draw-on precedent, money arriving the way it accrued. On pull-to-refresh, one dot per lane drifts along its ribbon (the animation above) — CALayer offset-path, the BerryRain class, because decorative motion during a refresh must be CoreAnimation. Never both at once: one animation per moment.

Liquidity v2 · the range reads as a range

The bar's grammar, fixed: a recessed track is the whole price world, the raised tinted segment is YOUR range, and the white dot is the price now. In range: the dot sits inside the lit segment and the segment carries a slow earning shimmer — alive because it's working. Out of range: the segment goes cold gray, the dot sits outside it wearing attention, and the gap between dot and segment IS the picture. V1's dot-at-the-end read as a slider handle; this can't.

Fees are the delight: uncollected fees are the one number here that's money arriving, so they wear confirm and count up on mount (numericText) — while a $0-fee row stays quiet gray, because zero earning is not a green moment. Pair icons replace the "UN" monogram (already in code); rows tighten to title + one subline + the 14pt track.

Law check: no hairlines — the track is a recessed well, the dot's ring is the card showing through, the range segment is tone; color stays identity (wallet tint, pair brands), state (attention dot, cold segment), magnitude (port heights, ribbon widths — in wears confirm because arriving money is the app's green moment, out stays ink per the "Owed" ruling); sentence case; motion is 250ms-family, one animation per moment, and anything that runs during a refresh is CALayer (the BerryRain lesson). The shimmer is ambient state ("earning"), not a transition, and is killed by Reduce Motion along with the drift dots and the draw-on.
