Spending pace

You never set a budget: last month's total is the bar to beat. Behind this month's running line, Spending draws last month's, day by day across the whole month, and one line of words says how far you are from its total. The chart spans the whole window, so today's dot stands at today. A dashed outline marks what's new; every other part is the box as it ships (§1468, §1480 item 14, §1526).

Rulings it touches

  • §1468 item 1, amended. The box gets a third line: the last window's whole total, as what's left to match it or how far past it you are. It's absent when the last window had nothing.
  • §1480 item 14, amended. The running line spans the whole window and stops at today. Behind it is the last window's running line, grey, with no fill and no dot.
  • §1524 holds. An account picked in the search tray compares against its own last window, as the line above already does.
  • Not drawn, on purpose: "on pace for $X" (a projection, §83), a budget you type in (setup is a cost), limits per place, and a notification when you pass last month.

Cost

  • No new fetch: the cached charges already reach two months back (SpendingSource.refresh), which covers last month whole and last week whole.
  • Spending.Reading gains lastWhole and lastRunning.
  • TokenChartPlot gains an optional second series and a fixed x span. Every other chart passes neither and draws exactly as it does today.
