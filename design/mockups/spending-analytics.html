Spending · against last month

The Spending page as it stands draws this month by place against last month to the same day. This adds three things the app can compute from what it already holds: the comparison (this month against last month to the same day, as the Home panel already says it), the recurring half from Subscriptions and Billers so the rest is the number people want, and cuts by event, person and fee. Everything is arithmetic over rows already in the store. No budget, no goal, no model writing a sentence. A dashed outline marks what's new.

Rules the mockup implies

  • The comparison is last month to the same day, the rule Spending already has; a week compares to the week before. No day: a day is a list, and it is already on Home and in the Feed. Never a budget, never a figure the user set.
  • "About the same" is within 10%. Under that, no sign and no word (§83: a change that rounds to nothing has no mark). Over or under names the dollars, not a percent.
  • Recurring is what Subscriptions and Billers already believe. Nothing new is classified; the rest is the remainder.
  • Events are calendar events that span a day or more or carry a location; a charge inside the event's hours is the event's. A charge is in one event at most, the shorter one.
  • People match an Addresses entry to an onchain counterparty, a Wise recipient, or a receipt or chat that names the split. The "you're owed" line comes from a split the person wrote down, never inferred.
  • Fees are gas from the Wallet rows' fee fields, Wise's quoted fee, and the card's own fee line. Nothing estimated.
  • Receipts match by merchant, amount within a dollar, and three days. One mail to one charge. An unmatched charge says "No receipt in your mail".
  • Hide balances masks every figure here, the "more than September" words included.
  • No colour on money, no chart. The box is a statement; the rows are the data. Range words through DSRangeChips(slim:); rows are SubscriptionRow; the receipt lines are the mail's own words, never a model's.
