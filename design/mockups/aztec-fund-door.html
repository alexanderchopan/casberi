Money into Aztec: the door

Casberi wires no bridge into Move. Under Private in Holdings, one row opens Shield's page in Aztec's browser, where the co-signer signs, the score is checked and the deposit is made by Shield's own code. The row is a link, not plumbing: when a better bridge comes, one line changes. Dashed outlines mark what is new; everything else is as drawn today.

What the tap does

  • Opens shield.human.tech in Aztec's browser (AztecBrowser, the §1700c link door) with your Aztec account connected and, on Ethereum, the "Use your co-signer instead" row ready (§1700l). Shield's page signs in the co-signer, shows the score, takes the deposit from the co-signer, and asks your Aztec account to claim. Casberi draws only the band and the ask sheets it already draws for any app.
  • Stands only when the Aztec account exists and is shown (AztecAccountStore.isShown), on All and Yours, never on a followed wallet's page.
  • Not a Move sentence, not Shield's SDK, no score check of our own. Swapping the bridge later is the URL and the small word.
