Markets: the categories in the box, coloured by the day

Today's screen, then the proposal with Watchlist selected, then with Social selected. Demo figures, made up for the mockup. The glyphs stand in for the real SF Symbols. A category's colour is the average day move of the listed companies behind its apps; "Not traded" companies (Anthropic, Bluesky) don't count.

What it costs, and what it changes
  • Your watchlist's heat map leaves the box. It becomes one tile, the Watchlist tile, coloured by your stocks' average move. Its list keeps the Movers order, so the biggest move still leads, just below the box rather than in it.
  • The All tile (every company, §1082) goes. Search already finds any company or app. It could come back as an "A–Z" row at the top of Search.
  • The colours are already paid for. Each category's index page prices its companies and draws this heat map today (CompanyQuotes.load(pack)), and the All page prices every company. The box would load them all at landing instead of on a tap: one read, about 60 quotes.
  • Hide balances doesn't hide this. These are market moves, not your money, so the colours stay.
