Subscriptions by category

This lives inside the Subscriptions group of Coming up, below the calendar, so you reach it by scrolling. The group opens with its monthly total and a map of where that money goes, then the rows. The map is the Holdings treemap reused, with categories in place of tokens: true-area tiles, grey, lit blue when pressed.

The map is a filter, not a picture. Pressing a category narrows the rows under it to that category, and the total reads that category. Pressing again clears it. That's why it sits above the rows rather than below them: what it controls is right under your thumb.
Where it gets thin. A category comes from matching the merchant to an app in the catalogue (BillersSource.category(ofMerchant:)). Anything billed through the App Store reads "Apple" and can't be placed. If most of someone's money is in Other, the map is one big grey tile and says nothing. We should count that on real data first. If Other is more than about half, hold the map until there's a better way to match.
