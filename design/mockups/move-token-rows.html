Move, private: one proposal

You never pick a protocol. On Ethereum there is one private place, your private balance: ETH and USDC go to Privacy Pools, any other token goes to Railgun, decided by the token. Aztec is a separate network, picked like Base or Ethereum. The protocol is named only in the foot.

Receive, the same way

Today Receive has a Private switch: on, it gives a one-time fresh address whose money moves into your private balance. Proposed: no switch. Where it lands is the pick, as in Send, with your private balance among the places.

Compared with today: the To list's two rows already exist ("Your private balance · Ethereum", "Your private account · Aztec"); Aztec's becomes "Your private balance" too, told apart by its network. Sending from private money stops being a fake network pick ("Ethereum · Private") and becomes private token rows. Holdings keeps the protocol name at the end of its lines, because there it is a fact, not a choice.
