Wallet watchlist

The watchlist is yours: tokens you want to keep an eye on, apart from the wallets you follow. It stands open on Wallet Home, so you see it the moment you open the Wallet: five tokens, then a door to the rest. Markets doesn't come back: there's no tile, seat, sector board, alert or widget. A dashed outline marks what's new.

Rulings it touches

  • §1529, amended. The watchlist comes back as a door on Home. The Markets tile, the tokens seat, sectors, the company index, price alerts and the Watchlist widget stay deleted.
  • §1480 item 2. Home lists the watchlist instead of putting it behind a door. "Every list behind a door" gets one exception, because a watchlist only works if you can see it at a glance.
  • §1120, the verbs. That ruling says Follow is the verb for everything you keep up with. "Watchlist" and "Watch a token" need an exception written into §1120. The alternative is "Follow a token", which would put us back on the word you just separated from Following.

Cost (unmeasured)

  • Tokens you hold are already priced by Holdings' read.
  • Tokens you don't hold need one batched Zerion fungibles call. It runs when Home or the Watchlist page comes on screen, at most once per ten-minute holdings window (§216), and never in the background sweep. Between reads, Home shows the last prices; a reading older than the window says when it's from. Ceiling: a person who keeps the Wallet open makes six calls an hour.
  • The chart is one charts call per range, made only when the token's page is open.
