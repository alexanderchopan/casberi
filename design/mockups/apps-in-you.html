Apps in You

Apps is the one list of everything you can connect, with what you have marked. It moves out of Settings into the tray's You row. Prices stay in the Wallet. An app's page links to its price there. Built from option 2 of settings-connected-options, with "Added" in place of "Connected".

The list

One A–Z list, every option visible. Added · 9 sits at the end of the search bar; the glass bar stays categories, in Dock order.

The two pages around it

What doesn't change: the Wallet's Coming up (calendar, Subscriptions group, Add a subscription), billers in Addresses, and a room's sliders button, which still opens that app's page.
Rule: you add a thing where its defining fact lives. A connection in Apps, a price in the Wallet, a person in Addresses. One entry shows in the others as a link, never a second copy.
