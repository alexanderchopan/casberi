Holdings: the sections

The same demo money three ways. The box, the tiles and Positions · Apps · NFTs are unchanged and left out of the frames. Every row's line already reads network · fact · place; the question is what the section heads say, and whether Lent is a section or a fact on a row, now that private money is lent too.

Reading them

  • Two axes, one list. Where the money is (public or private) and what it is doing (idle, earning). A section can carry one axis; the row's line carries the other. A says state for public money and place for private money, which is the muddle. B puts WHERE on the section and WHAT on the line, everywhere. C puts WHAT on the section and WHERE on the line.
  • Which figure matters at the head. B keeps "how much is private" (the number the account's design is built around, §1700g). C keeps "how much is earning" instead. Move's Lend already draws the lent total for the Lend verb, so B loses less.
  • Positions · Apps · NFTs are the same under all three and are not shown.
