Safe and Private pages

Your account is a Safe, and your private balance is 0xBow's Privacy Pools v2, so picking either app should show your money in it, not a stranger's reading. Today the Safe pick draws the same token treemap as All and never says "Safe", and the Privacy Pools pick draws an empty box. The proposal: Safe answers "who can move this money, and what is waiting on me?" and Privacy Pools answers "how much is private, what is cleared, and how hidden is it?" Open questions
