No strip on a fresh connection, and that is the design. The backfill is six days on Linea and one on Base, so a new install cannot know a prior 30-day window. knowsPriorWindow refuses the comparison instead of estimating it — the note says so in words rather than showing a percentage the room never observed.

No colour on the change, no arrow. Spending more is not a failure and spending less is not a win; the app has no idea which the person wanted.

No merchants. Structurally impossible — the merchant never reaches the chain. This is the one card a payment card cannot draw.

Currency rows only when there is more than one. A legend of one row is a restatement, so a single-currency card gets the headline and the strip alone.

A WETH spend has no currency, so it lands in unpriced and is counted rather than summed as zero.
