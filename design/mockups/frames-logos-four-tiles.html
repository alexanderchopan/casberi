Frames and Logos in four tiles

Each room keeps its four views as tiles. The verbs move out under one rule, the same one the Wallet's Watch follows: a verb that adds an account is the first row of the Accounts menu, a verb that moves money leads the Holdings list, and a door to a web page stays on the room's own page (the sliders disc). Dashed outlines mark what moved. The figures are the demo's.
