Adding cash through Peer

Dollars from Revolut, Zelle or Cash App become USDC on Base in your account, bought from a person on Peer (peer.xyz). Casberi draws every step in Move's words; the only page that is not ours is the payment app's own sign-in, inside our sheet. Peer is the contract underneath, never a screen. What stands under it

  • No custody. Peer's contract holds the seller's USDC and releases it straight to your account. Casberi holds no money and keeps no Revolut sign-in.
  • Venmo and PayPal are grey, never missing, because Peer asks for a stake equal to the order and a new account has none.
  • Cost: about two weeks for the first app, a Swift port of Peer's proof flow (3–5 days of it the encryption), seen with real money on a phone. Review risk: a payment app's sign-in in our web view, as Peer's own iOS app does. Not ours to control: Peer's prices, sellers and fee, and Revolut's page.
