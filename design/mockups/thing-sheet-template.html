Thing sheets as rooms: one card, four tiles

Every sheet in the easy group gets the same frame a room has: a head row, the room's box at its fixed size (236pt), the tiles 15pt under it (52pt each, the same corner and fill as You's row), then the facts and body. The dial of round buttons becomes the tiles. Demo data; the glyphs stand in for the real SF Symbols the dial already uses.

What this asks you to decide
  • Tile ink. Shown white, like You's places. Rooms draw a verb tile (Notes' New) in blue; on a sheet every tile is a verb, so a row of four blue tiles is the alternative.
  • Pin into Share's menu when there are five. The other choice is dropping Name or Copy from money receipts, the only sheet that reaches five today besides World App transfers.
  • Three tiles split three ways, not four with a gap. Rooms already do this when a place has fewer tiles.
  • The rest of the easy group follows the same three shapes: purchase receipt, picture (cropped to the box, tap for whole), social person, Privy app, L2BEAT and Walletbeat reports, agent grant.
  • Not in this pass: chart, article, post and mail (a statement card), and the prose sheets (notes, conversations).
