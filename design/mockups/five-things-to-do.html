Five things to do

Notes and sharing are the two places you make something from what you kept. These five add to that, each on parts the app already draws: the note sheet's tools, a note's page with its three keys, the Notes room's folders, the share card. Phones are the running app's geometry; glyphs are the real SF Symbols. Order is the order I'd build them.

1 · Highlights

Select a passage on a reading page, and Keep stands in the selection menu beside Copy. Keeping makes a note of yours: the passage as its words, linked to the thing it came from. From there it is a note like any other, so the room, folders, the long press, the lock and the card all take it with nothing new.

To rule on. (1) The selection menu: SwiftUI's own text selection offers no custom item, so the reading body (the article, the post, the mail) becomes a UITextView-backed reader to carry Keep. One component, every reading page. (2) Storage: the passage rides quote (the field an X notice's post already uses), the link to the thing rides wikilinks (§982.4), your own words under it ride content. No new CloudKit field. (3) A highlight's row in the Notes room shows the article's name as its second line, where a list shows "1 of 3 done".

2 · A folder shares as one card

A folder already holds notes and the things you pinned (§980). Its long press gains Share beside Rename and Delete, and the card lists what is in it. The folder's name is the statement, the count and the day are the eyebrow, and the rows carry each thing's mark and title.

To rule on. (1) Five rows on the card, newest first; the eyebrow says the true count ("8 things"), so there is no "and 3 more" line. (2) A folder has no link, so the share's second item is the card's text representation: the name, then one line per thing, which Notes, Mail and a text field take (ShareCardPart.card(words:) already carries words). (3) A folder holding a locked note lists it as "Locked note" (§982.6), never its words.

3 · A checklist item becomes a reminder

A list you tick (§982.1) ends there today. Hold an item on the note's page and Remind me raises one solid sheet with three times. The reminder lands in Reminders, in a list named Casberi, with the note as its door; the item shows when it will ring at its trailing edge.

To rule on. (1) Direction: the app makes the reminder (an EventKit write with the system's own consent, the outcome flashed, never a silent try?). Ticking the item in Casberi completes the reminder it made; completing it in Reminders ticks the item on the next read, since the Reminders room already reads what is done. That is the app's own reminder in both directions, not a write into someone else's data. (2) Three fixed times and a picker, no more: Tonight 6:00 PM, Tomorrow 9:00 AM, Pick a time. (3) The hold is the item's own; the page's long press elsewhere stays the note's five.

4 · A sketch in a note

The writing page's Attach menu gains Sketch under Choose a photo and Scan a document. It opens Apple's canvas with Apple's tool picker; pulling the canvas down keeps the drawing as the note's one picture (§974), so the row, the lede and the page draw it with nothing new.

To rule on. (1) §982 declined sketching with Math Notes, tables and tags. This reverses that one item; the other declines stand. (2) A sketch is a picture, kept as the drawing's bitmap in previewImageData at the app's one 480pt size. The strokes themselves are not kept: a new CloudKit field would be a separate Production ship, and a sketch opened again draws over the kept picture as its background, which is how Apple's canvas takes an image. (3) A picture and a recording never share a sheet (§974); a sketch is a picture, so the same holds. (4) The Mac has the canvas but no tool picker; the pointer draws and the Mac gets a pen and an eraser as two keys.

5 · A voice note shares as a card, with the recording

Today a voice note's Share sends the card with its transcript and no sound. The card gains the player's waveform and length (§987.3), and the share sheet gets the recording as a second item, so a target that takes files sends the voice and a post target sends the card.

To rule on. (1) The second item is the kept .m4a written to a temporary file, named after the note; ShareCardPart gains .audio(URL) beside .link. Messages, Mail, AirDrop and Files take it; a post target ignores it. (2) The card draws the first sentence as the statement and the rest of the words under it, clamped to the card. The length stands at the waveform's end. (3) The bars on the card are the player's 32, all solid: a card has no playhead.
