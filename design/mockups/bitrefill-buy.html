Buy · a gift card from Move

Bitrefill's API is headless (products, an invoice with an address and an exact amount, the code once it is paid), so nothing of Bitrefill's is drawn. Buy is Move's fourth verb (§1438, §1438a): the same sentence with two words of its own, the category and the merchant, each opening its choices under it, the same Send signing the invoice (§1436's path), and the code lands as the order thing §103 already draws as a receipt. A dashed outline marks what's new.

Rules the mockup implies

  • Buy is a verb of Move, not a room or a tile. It appears only when a Bitrefill key is connected; without one the verbs' line stays three, and the Bitrefill offer in Sources gains the one sentence "Buy gift cards from Move". A verb that cannot act is not drawn (§83).
  • The key is the person's own. Their Bitrefill account creates the invoice and receives the code; Casberi holds no key, no code, no balance. This amends §103's offer line from "nothing here ever buys" to "buys only what you sign for".
  • Paying is §1436's Send. The invoice names an address, an exact amount and a network; the sentence's "with" lists only tokens Bitrefill takes (USDC, ETH, BTC via the Lightning seat later), and "on" only the networks it takes them on. Bitrefill takes USDC on Base (usdc_base), Arbitrum, Ethereum, Polygon and Solana (its crypto-payments docs and help centre, read 2026-10-22), so "on Base." stands; the invoice's own list is read live and the network blank offers only those.
  • The amount is the product's. Fixed denominations snap; a ranged product takes any figure in its range; the field opens in dollars and the grey line shows the token. No slider: a card is not a share of what you have.
  • The quote expires. The grey line counts the invoice down; past it, Buy greys and the line says "Ask Bitrefill again" (a tap re-quotes). A send after expiry is refused before Face ID, never after.
  • The category word is Bitrefill's categories, folded to seven: Shopping (Online Marketplaces, Department Stores, Multi-Brand, Fashion, Electronics, Home & DIY, Health & Beauty, Sports & Outdoors, Gifts, Kids, Pets), Food (Food & Groceries), Travel (Travel, Transportation, Gasoline), Games, Entertainment, Phone (VoIP, top-ups, eSIMs), Money (Payment Cards, Crypto, Privacy & Tools). Never Casberi's category names: a card is a spend and lands in Wallet whatever it is for. The word is optional; a merchant picked first fills it.
  • Landing is the §103 shape. One new thing per order, the receipt anatomy, the code as a copy row, Redeem as the product's link. Nothing claims Used or an expiry Bitrefill does not report.
  • Hosts are declared already (api-bitrefill.com, www.bitrefill.com under the Bitrefill reach); the purpose line changes to say it also creates invoices you pay.
