One search: the tray finds everything you have

The face's tray is the only search. It finds everything you've kept by name: apps, accounts, people, feeds, calendars, mail lists, subscriptions, notes and watched companies. Results come in sections, starting with the place you opened it from, and the last row hands the words to Find. A place's capsule keeps only its filters and its Add. Demo data. The glyphs stand in for the real SF Symbols.

The rule, and what it changes
  • The tray searches what you have, and a capsule only adds. Finding something you haven't added yet (a company, an app from the catalogue) stays in that place's Add, because the tray can't hold the whole world.
  • Settings' bar is deleted. It held Search alone (SettingsScope.bar), so with Search gone the face stands alone. The box's counts are still its filter (§1138).
  • Notes becomes All · Folders · Voice · New. NotesSearchSheet is deleted, and the tray takes its matching on titles and words.
  • Markets: Search becomes Add (plus, the New tile's glyph), opening the same company search. Not Watch, because eye already means the Watchlist tile.
  • The Apps catalogue keeps its field. It's the add flow itself, a sheet you open to connect something new.
  • Amends §1133e (the tray finds kinds, not just names), §1138 item 6 (Markets' bar) and the Notes tiles (§1099/§1127).
  • Cost: the tray's search reads notes' words, so it moves off the main actor. That's the same fetch Find already runs.
