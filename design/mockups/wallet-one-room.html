One Wallet: every money room on one page

Today each money app you connect is its own room, and you swipe through them inside the Wallet category. Merged, the category is the room, with one total and one feed. It uses the same template that Wallet, Frames and Logos got today (§1039): the box, the tiles, the account menu, then the list. Frames and Logos stay their own rooms. What it costs: (1) A source's own head stops being a room head. Stripe's revenue crown becomes a tile figure, as Safe's queue becomes Coming up. (2) Swiping walks the nine categories instead of the rooms, and the tray lists only categories. (3) Work has the most kinds, so it's the room most likely to need more than eight tiles. (4) The catalogue's 16 groups and the dock's 9 categories have to become the same list. (5) A source with no kind of its own (a lone RSS feed) shows up only as a menu entry.
