Money receipt in the room's frame

The party moves up into the sheet's head, the way every sheet leads. The box holds what happened: the amount on one line at one size, what it was worth, then its facts as rows (status, network, fee). The head already says who and what, so no sentence repeats it. Then three or four tiles, then your last three with that person as rows, and a door to all of them. Name is no longer a tile: tapping the face or name opens the address card where you name it, and an address with no name says so in the head. Demo data; glyphs stand in for SF Symbols.

What changes
  • The box is a room's box: a statement in white (the amount, number and token together), one quiet line (worth and when), then a two-by-two grid with the value over its label, as Settings' counts stand. The sentence that restated the head is deleted, and the stamp is the Status cell.
  • The history is rows, not bars: the last three with this party, each opening its own sheet, then "All 15 with maya.eth ›" to their address card. The card's "your 12th from them" line goes; the count lives in the door.
  • The fourth tile keeps up with it for you: Save a person (Addresses), Watch a wallet you saved, Track a charge that repeats. The same slot elsewhere: Follow a feed or post, Watch a token or stock. Naming happens on the address card that Save and the head both open.
  • The receipt's torn edge goes: a room's box has no tear. Its job, saying a record is final, is the stamp's.
