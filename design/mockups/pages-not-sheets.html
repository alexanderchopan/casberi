Day, Notes and Sources as pages

Today Day and Notes rise as one kind of sheet and Sources as another, Settings stacks inside Sources, and What this app reaches inside Settings. Here every tile in the Feed's row is a page with the band under it, so search lives in one place and the title says where you are. The word before the dot is grey and takes you back; the band's face is always the way home.

Day and Notes work the same way: "Feed · Day", "Feed · Notes", the tile row with theirs picked, the band underneath. A note you open is still its full page, and system sheets (Share, Safari, pickers) still rise, as everywhere.
Not in this change: the Wallet's own tiles (Holdings, Security, Markets) still open as sheets; they could follow the same rule next.
