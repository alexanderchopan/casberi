What shipped

  1. A dateline above the lede. The whisper capsule that opens this screen says "Your Friday"; the screen itself named the day nowhere. One tertiary line, locale-formatted (Friday, July 31 / Freitag, 31. Juli), so a brief re-opened at 4pm still reads as today's.
  2. A lede rung of its own — heading28. It was heading22, which is what every card title and tray header wears, so the sentence and the hero's price40 under it — same rounded bold face, ten points apart — read as one label-over-value unit. 34 was the other option and it's wrong the opposite way: a real lede runs four lines there and pushes the money hero, the crown by ruling, off the first screen. One caller only, the way price48 is one caller.
  3. The figure wears its direction. The synthesis notes already color signed percentages; the day's biggest number, in the sentence most likely to carry it, was the app's only uncolored move. The composer hands the renderer the exact substring it built the sentence from, so the color can never disagree with the words — and a rung that isn't a gain or loss (a health factor, a handle, a deadline) sends no direction and stays in body ink.
  4. Spacing as structure. Every module self-padded the same 10pt, drawing the brief as one undifferentiated column. Stack now takes an optional second argument naming the ids that open a chapter, and the brief composes it — so the money story, the day's subject, the agent's read and the things themselves separate at 28/28/28/36pt while what's glued stays glued: the watchlist to the hero (ruled, one story never split), and the second lead to the first. No headers, no rules, no hairlines. Absent on the other ~90 Stack emitters, where it's a no-op.
  5. The leads get the room the doctrine promises. "A lead is one item given room" — but the read was a 48pt thumbnail with a two-line title that cut mid-word, and the mention was 16pt text in a gutter 32pt narrower than the card. The read's art now leads as a 128pt banner at the card's own width with an 18pt title on three lines; the mention's avatar moves to a header row so the words get the full width, at body17, the app's reading rung, up to five lines (the composer already clamps the post to 200 characters upstream, so that's bounded).
