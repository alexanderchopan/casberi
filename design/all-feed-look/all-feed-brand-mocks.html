C · Uber — typographic utility

White ground, black chrome, hierarchy carried entirely by type weight: 20pt-800 section heads, tight radii, monochrome icon tiles, a chevron promising every row is a door. Color retreats to the brand tiles alone. This is the app's own light mode, disciplined harder.

Tension: counts return INTO the sentence (Uber's list grammar), giving back the trailing-figure move you just shipped. Strongest hierarchy of the three; least money-voice.
