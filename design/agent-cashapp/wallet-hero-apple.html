Casberi · the wallet hero, as Apple would design it

Stocks' chart, Health's cards, no blue rectangle

Apple has never once shipped a balance inside a coloured card — Apple Card's balance sits on a plain surface, Stocks' quote sits on black. Their grammar for "here is a number that moves": the figure as the monument, the direction as a sentence in semantic ink, and then the biggest element on the screen is the chart itself, tall and touchable with a gradient falling away beneath the line. Colour comes from the DATA (green because it's up today, red when it isn't) — never from a container.

What changed from ours: the blue card is gone — the number sits on black at 54pt with its cents faded, the way Apple Card writes money. Direction is a green sentence ("▲ $224.51 (1.8%) today"), not a pill. The chart grows from a 52pt strip into the screen's dominant element — gradient under the line, scrub cursor, day axis — because for Apple the chart IS the product. The range control becomes Stocks' compact segmented box. Below, the room's readings arrive as Health-style measurement cards: Deposited and Approvals half-width (orange only on the risk figure), Holdings keeping the treemap inside a card with a door. The wallet identity lives where it already lives — the face rail — and colour is exclusively semantic: green because it's up. On a down day this entire screen would be red-accented, which is the honesty Apple's grammar buys.
