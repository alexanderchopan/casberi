Should the Wallet get this flavor?

Yes in exactly two places, no everywhere else. The wallet room is the one screen in the app that IS a Cash App screen — one hero number over one list (§212 built exactly that) — so the balance hero is the most natural home the bright register has: full tint ground, white figure at display scale, the white delta pill it already shares with the day card. And when the room is SCOPED to one wallet, the hero wears that wallet's own pour hue instead of the tint — which is colour as information (which wallet am I looking at), the same fact the crown pour already states, now stated where the money is.

The refusals matter more. Approvals, the exposure card, DeFi health, the acting-parties inventory — the risk surfaces — stay ink. A bright card on a screen that says "this grant reaches $4,120" dresses a warning as a celebration; orange stays a semantic accent on ink, per §83. The transactions list stays ink for the feed's own reason (content is the product), and the treemap keeps its magnitude blues — it already carries colour that means something. The face rail's selection goes solid, same rule as the chips.
