Stocktwits — the ticker is the mark

A stock has no coin art, so the mark is a round ticker monogram disc — a coin without art, honest to the material (it never fakes a logo) and matching the ruled grammar: assets are round. The subline keeps the row's existing facts (exchange · Stocktwits' own watcher count); the NEW fact is the right column: live price + day change from the Yahoo read the chart sheet already does, now surfaced at the row. "Latest takes" drops — the feed shows what landed, a manager manages.

The price column costs one Yahoo fetch per watched ticker per visit — same endpoint and UA the chart already uses, batched on appear. Flat reads gray, no direction.

RULE 1 · THE MARK — RULED "circles"Assets are round, topics are square

Your ruling, 2026-07-23: the §182 grammar refines to round = a person or an asset (coin art, or a ticker disc standing in for it), square = a topic (channel, feed, publication, contract glyph). No round art ever gets clipped into a squircle, and no stock ever wears a fake coin. Recorded in prd with this pass.

RULE 2 · THE LIVE FACT — RULED "roster"Price and delta ride under the coin

Each slot carries the asset's one live fact: Tokens reads the 24h pulse it already caches for the Movers sort (TokenPulse — the same cache the feed reads, so the two can never disagree); Stocktwits fetches the Yahoo day quote the chart sheet already uses. A price we couldn't reach renders nothing — never a dash, which reads like a number. Flat is gray, signless, directionless (§83).

RULE 3 · SAME CUTS AS §182Furniture out, recents out

Header coin card → gone (nav subtitle carries the promise); "Watch a token / Watch a stock" section headers → gone (the omnibox says it); Stocktwits' "Latest takes" preview → gone per your recents ruling. Order modes, swipe-to-unwatch, the swipe coach, and the row-tap chart sheets all survive untouched.

What doesn't change

Every honesty line survives: no invented per-ticker sentiment (Bullish/Bearish stays per-POST, on the things in the feed, where it's the author's own call); watcher counts stay labeled as Stocktwits' scale, not endorsement; read-only stated once. The thing-IS-the-watch model (unwatch deletes the thing) is untouched underneath.

Law check: no hairlines; sentence case; the washes are each bridge's brand hue. The price column introduces no new color rules — up/down/flat already exist in TokenChartStyle. The one new doctrine is RULE 1's refinement of §182, which I'll record in prd with the ruling. After your call I build both screens in one pass — they share the row anatomy, so it's one component worn twice.
