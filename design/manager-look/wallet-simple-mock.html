Below the shelf — four slabs, one sentence

Field, button, promise, two doors. The field's Watch lives INSIDE it (one slab, not field-plus-pill). The address book stops being a headed section with a paragraph and a blue text link — it's a door wearing its count, exactly like Connection wears its chains. Every explainer paragraph moved behind the door it explained.

What made it feel busy — the census

ShapesBefore: recessed field + side pill + full capsule + card rows + bare text link + gear row — 6 shapes. After: the slab and the circle — 2. One radius (18), one height (56), so the eye reads a rhythm instead of a collage.
FontsBefore: body text, subheads, labels, capsule label — mixed weights of the text face. After: the money font (rounded, bold) for anything tappable; the text face only for the one gray sentence. Tappable = rounded-bold becomes a rule you can feel.
WordsBefore: three footer paragraphs (~70 words) always on screen. After: one nine-word sentence. "Names cost nothing, land nothing…" moves inside the Address book door where the naming actually happens; the chains footer already lives in Connection.
VerbsUnchanged — watch, connect, name, configure. Nothing lost; two of the four just stand behind their counts now.

Law check: no hairlines (the slab's edge is a fill, not a rule); sentence case; the one blue block is the screen's primary verb, not decoration; both doors state a real fact ("4 names", "All 7 chains") — no dead chrome. The Cash App borrow is the geometry and the discipline, not the branding: tint stays Casberi blue, the money font is the system's own rounded face (ui-rounded / SF Rounded), already what the nav titles use.
