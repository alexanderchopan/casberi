    My read, and the calls for your ruling

    RULE 1 · STATE 1, REORDEREDPayoff → act → help, same components

    Pre-connect keeps every existing part but inverts the order: the ghost preview leads (the screen opens as a demo), the field is the one act, and the steps demote — step 1 becomes a link capsule opening the exact page, the rest collapse behind "Show the steps ›". Keyless bridges add a one-tap PEEK (the Wallet chip / Kalshi browse-first pattern). Ask: ratify the reorder?

    RULE 2 · THE FLIPConnected, the form retires

    The moment a connection verifies, the manager state takes the screen: identity → proof pill → can-do sentences → one ⚙︎ door (replace credentials, disconnect). Ask: ratify the flip as the standing template?

    RULE 3 · ONE COMPONENT, NOT THREEUnify with BridgeDetailScreen

    The connected state above is 90% of what BridgeDetailScreen already renders (proof, can-do, Reconnect/Pause/Remove). Building it inside each setup screen would make a third copy. The clean move: ONE shared connected-manager component; the setup screen renders it in state 2, and the bridge-detail route renders the same thing. Ask: unify, or keep the two surfaces separate?

    RULE 4 · TIER 1 FIRSTOrder of operations

    (1) TokenSetupScreen's flip — one rework, eleven bridges inherit. (2) Shopify's ledger — nearly free on the RSS components. (3) Tier 2 one-offs adopt the shared state: Steam, Mail, Twitch, Spotify, Exchange, Obsidian. Kalshi's light odds-column pass and the chain pickers' furniture cuts trail as a sweep.

    What doesn't change

    Every honesty line survives: keys in Keychain only, read-only stated in the can-do sentences (each bridge's own words, not boilerplate), the §163 exchange verdict, the ghost preview's "not yet real" dimming. GitHub keeps its repo/feed follows — they render as topic rows under the connected state, the §182 grammar.

    Law check: no hairlines; sentence case; washes stay each bridge's brand hue; glass stays floating. The proof pill is the one green element and it reports a real sync fact, never decoration — a bridge that hasn't synced yet shows no pill rather than a fake one (the no-fake-status rule). The ⚙︎ door follows the wallet's §139 amendment precedent: credentials are set-once configuration, not a read.
