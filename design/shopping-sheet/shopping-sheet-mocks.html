Shopping · thing sheets · 12 Aug 2026

Every shopping sheet keeps its money in a sentence

Five seats — Shopify, Deals, Open Food Facts, Bitrefill, Privacy.com — land eight row shapes between them. Not one has a sheet anatomy of its own. Every one falls through to the generic path: a link-preview card, and the label/value grid the user called a database field.

The inventory turned up the same disease the wallet and Work passes found, in its purest form yet. Three bridges stamp a real priceValue and no row in the app draws it. Two bridges join the amount into the title string at ingest, where nothing can total it. All three product bridges stamp the store, the publisher or the brand into authorHandle — and it is rendered nowhere in the app: not in the row, not in the sheet.

Four diseases, stated once — every one of them is a fact the record already holds.
  • Money is either prose you can't total or a number you can't see. Bitrefill and Privacy join the amount into the title at ingest. Shopify, Deals and Open Food Facts land a real priceValue, and no feed row in this app draws that field — its only appearance anywhere is one unlabelled heading17 line in the .product sheet branch.
  • authorHandle is stamped by all three product bridges and drawn nowhere. BandRow.project has cases for Bluesky, Farcaster, Nostr, Slack, RSS, GitHub, Peer and the wallets — and falls to nil for every Shopping seat. So the store, the publisher and the brand are in the store, in the index, in the demo seed, and on no screen.
  • From — from a store you follow is the spec table's whole contribution, and it is wrong for two of the three sources that get it. A deal comes from a feed publisher; a scan comes from your own hand.
  • The link-preview card is doing a product photo's job. LPMetadataProvider re-scrapes a page the record already has art and a title for — and for two Bitrefill shapes it scrapes an account page every row shares. The one card that renders is the one card that can be wrong.

2The ruling — two archetypes, not five

A shopping thing is one of exactly two things, and the fork is did money leave. It is a data test, not a source list — the §363 correction, applied before it can be re-broken: priceValue != nil && the kind is .transaction or the ref names an order, so a seat that ships next month is covered the day it lands.
A receipt (Privacy card purchase, Bitrefill order, Bitrefill refill — and the same anatomy the Wallet card seats already want) leads with the amount: money is the hero rung, the merchant is the subject, the rail is a slab row, the date is the date it happened. A watch card (Shopify product, deal, food scan) leads with the thing: the picture at size, the store named with its own mark, the price with its history beside it, the state as a word.

The one rule that carries over from Work, unchanged

Never parse the title back. Privacy and Bitrefill both hold the merchant only inside a joined string, and the fix is at the bridge, not at a parser: stamp authorHandle with merchant.descriptor / product.name at ingest, the way the three product bridges already do. §303 and §340 ruled on exactly this — a String(localized:) clause is written in whatever language the device was in when the row landed, so a sheet that splits a title apart silently reads wrong the day someone changes their language. Two of these clauses happen to be plain English literals today; that is luck, not a contract.

5What it takes

In the order the existing passes did it: fix what the bridge throws away first, then give the sheet something to draw.
  1. Stamp what the bridges already read. PrivacyBridge.txnThing holds merchant.descriptor and joins it into the title; BitrefillBridge.orderThing does the same with product.name. Both write it to authorHandle instead — no new field, no CloudKit deploy — and both heal on the dedupe hit, which Privacy's pass already does for exactly this reason. This is the move that makes the whole category possible without ever parsing a title back.
  2. Draw authorHandle in the row. BandRow.project gains its Shopping cases — store, publisher, brand. Five lines, and it fixes the feed at the same time as the sheet: right now these rows say nothing at all in their trailing slot.
  3. ShopStage, Foundation-only, compiled whole by a self-test. The WorkStage / SocialSheet shape: a Reading with archetype (receipt · watch), subject, amount, state, tone, derived from kind, priceValue, sourceRef and the English facet tags — never from the title. Every failure in this file renders as a perfectly good-looking sheet, which is the only reason any of these harnesses exist.
  4. The provenance sentence replaces the spec row. From — from a store you follow stands down wherever the sentence composes, exactly as §363 gated it for social. The sentence differs per seat because the truth does: you scanned this, Slickdeals posted this, charged to your Privacy card.
  5. The one stored field this needs, and the choice about it. Shopify's old price is overwritten on a drop; only the title's " (was €90.00)" survives it, and parsing that back is the thing rule 1 exists to prevent. Two honest options: (a) a new optional Thing.previousPrice — clean, and a CloudKit Production deploy before it ships, or it silently fails to mirror; (b) a price-history line on enrichedText, which already carries three documented exceptions to the retrieval-only rule, costs no deploy, and has the side benefit that "the pan was ninety, now seventy-two" becomes searchable. (b) unless we're deploying schema for something else anyway.
  6. Two things deliberately not built. A "was it still available?" check would mean Casberi fetching a store page on your behalf, and a stale in stock is the fake status the design law bans. And no total across Shopping seats: a Privacy dollar, a Bitrefill dollar and a Shopify euro are three different claims, and Corpus.cardSpendSources already refuses Privacy for exactly this class of reason.
The adjacency worth naming before we build. Privacy's card purchase is the same archetype as Apple Wallet, Gnosis Pay and ether.fi spends — which sit in Wallet, not Shopping. If the receipt anatomy here is built as ShopStage, four card seats end up with two different purchase sheets. The fork should be "did money leave", not "which catalog band is it in" — so this should almost certainly be PurchaseStage, serving all four, with the watch card as the Shopping-only half. Worth a ruling before the first line.
