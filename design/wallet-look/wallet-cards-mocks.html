E · The band — full bleed, no box

The map breaks the shape grammar instead of joining it: edge to edge, tight gaps, square shoulders — the one thing on the screen that is NOT a rounded island. In a crown full of capsules, that's what "distinguished" actually looks like; the color mass does the framing, no edge needed.

My read, for your ruling

The line: keep it free

§146 moved the balance OUT of a tile because the combined read is the crown feature and deserved "the room's headline voice — set on the page, no card." B and D re-litigate that with nothing new: the box adds an edge and subtracts authority, and it sits ON the pour, muting the one atmospheric move we just shipped. Cash App sets its big number free on the field too.

The map: not a box — a band

C's case died on the user's own observation: the crown is already a parade of capsules (source chips, switcher pills, sync capsule, delta pill, range chips), so one more rounded surface is camouflage, not distinction. When everything is an island, the standout move is breaking the shape grammar: E bleeds the map edge to edge — square shoulders, tight gaps, a stripe of color mass — the one thing on screen that is not an inset rounded object. That's also the most Cash App move on this page.

Engagement lives elsewhere

A box never made a screen engaging — confidence (price48), color (the pour, the brand cells), and motion (draw-on, marks, the retint) do that, and they shipped this morning. Cards decide composition, not excitement. If the room still doesn't say "oooh," the next lever is motion on the treemap cells, not a border around them.

Standing rulings touched: §146 (the balance left its tile for the headline voice — B/D reverse it), §151 (same), 2026-07-10 (treemap cells ARE cards — C nests them, so inside the box they'd flatten to washes), §158–159 (the pour; a top card sits on it). None of these block a ruling — they're what the ruling would amend.
