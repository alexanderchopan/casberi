C · Identity everywhere — tokens own their color

A gentler pour, and the treemap becomes the photograph: each token wears its own brand hue as the cell's wash — ETH indigo, USDC blue, SOL's gradient, WBTC orange. Identity color doing identity work, which is the color law's first job.

Risk: re-litigates 2026-07-10 ("colored fills read as noise") — but that ruling predates the magnitude wash and judged flat fills, not brand-hue gradients. Needs your eyes, which is what this frame is for.

Moves shared by all three (independent of the ruling)

The number grows a rung

46–50pt rounded instead of 40. Cash App's whole personality is numerals with confidence; ours are already SF Rounded — they're just shy.

Captions get quieter

The labels around the money drop a step in size and ink. Hierarchy comes from the gap between loud and quiet, and today's gap is too small.

The line gets body

2.2–2.6pt stroke, a real area fill, a bigger live endpoint. The current hairline-weight sparkline under a 40pt number reads like an afterthought.

Amounts join the stream

Transaction rows carry their USD trailing in rounded tabular figures — green only on receive. The stream starts reading like money instead of log lines.

What the law keeps in every frame: no hairlines, sentence case, glass only on the floating layer, and hue doing only its two jobs — identity (Wallet's blue, a wallet's face tint, a token's brand color) and state (the delta's green and red, refusing flat). None of these frames adds decorative color; they turn up voices the app already sanctions.
