What all three fix

Identity leads instead of section furniture; adding becomes the screen's primary act (an omnibox or a slot, not an errand card); chains/WalletConnect/disconnect collapse behind ONE ⚙︎ door — they're set-once plumbing and were charging rent on every visit. The §170 cap turns from copy into structure you can see.

The recommendation: A, with B's omnibox

The roster is the most Casberi — the Stories shelf is a grammar the chips already taught, faces are the identity system's payoff, and literal empty slots are the warmest honest way to show a cap. Its add field should behave like B's omnibox (find-or-add in one input). B is the purest tool if you want maximum sparseness; C is the best screenshot and the biggest swing.

What doesn't change

Every shipped fact and rule survives all three: detected kinds, round-vs-square marks, copy on every row, unlimited naming, the five-cap with grandfathering, "read-only" stated once in the subtitle instead of a footer paragraph.

Law check: no hairlines (the ledger's divider is a whisper of type, not a rule), sentence case throughout, glass only floating (the cards are fills), hue stays identity — every colored surface above is a wallet's own tint doing its one job. The ⚙︎ door needs a §139 amendment ("manage is one page, no doors") — the argument: §139 killed doors to READS, and connection plumbing isn't a read.
