The figure leads. The accounts are the third row above the dock, floating with no bar, as on Farcaster and Bluesky: the dock, the Wallet folder, then the faces. All is a slot like the others. Verbs are rows with the room's tint word under one label, Actions. Scoped to one account, the note reads “Main · …a013”.

Why it reads as four apps

  1. Four containers. A deck card, two act slabs, a grouped list, and the dock, at radii 18, 22, 14 and 26. The rest of the app just settled on one plate for rows and one head per room. This screen uses neither.
  2. The type scale runs backwards. "Send" is 34pt bold. The balance, the reason the room exists, is 22pt. Under the anatomy every other room got, the figure is the largest thing and a verb is a 17pt word.
  3. The faces at the top are a second identity strip. Two grey placeholder circles, "All accounts", then a run of address tails. It looks like a contacts header, it repeats the Accounts row below, and it sits where every other room puts its head. Picking an account belongs in one place.
  4. Fake status. "0 ETH ▲ 0 ETH (+100.0%)" over a flat line. Rule §83: a change that rounds to zero has no sign, and a chart with nothing in it isn't drawn.
  5. Verbs as slabs. §746 just moved every catalogue verb into its row. Send, Top up and Follow address are the same kind of thing, and they get the same shape.
  6. Three insets. The card is inset 16, the slabs 16, the list 20 with its rows inset again. One left edge, like the feed.
