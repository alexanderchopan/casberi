The composer

"who is 0x9a2E…" answers from the book; "mom's wallet" retrieves through it. Names become part of how you ask.

What stays honest: kind is detected (getCode → Contract, Safe service → Safe, watched → Watching), never a form the person fills. Naming costs zero network and lands nothing. Watching stays the deliberate upgrade, on the entry, with its cost stated ("the full feed"). Unwatching demotes back to the book — history wiped honestly, the name kept, because the name is the person's own data.
