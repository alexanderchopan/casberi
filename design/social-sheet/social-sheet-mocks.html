Social thing sheets: what they are today, and three ways to make them a receipt

Same move as the wallet sheets. A social sheet today is a title in display type, a body in secondary type, and a one-row spec table — a database record with the field names left on. Everything a post actually is (who, its pictures, what it quotes, how it landed, who liked it) is either buried below the fold or, for four of the seven sources, not drawn at all.

The inventory below is measured from source, not assumed. The headline finding: the sheet's social treatment is gated on SocialThread.isSocial, which is only Bluesky, Farcaster and Nostr — so an X, Instagram, TikTok or Snapchat post opens a sheet strictly worse than the row you tapped to get there.

The three things wrong, stated once:
  • The gate is a source list, not a data test. isSocialPost asks "is this Bluesky, Farcaster or Nostr?" when the question it means is "does this record carry an author and words?" Four sources stamp both and are refused.
  • The spec table is the database showing through. On a social post it renders exactly one row — From: saved by you — inside its own card, with an 80pt label column. It is the only thing on the sheet that looks like a form.
  • Reception is an afterthought. Three monospace numbers below the photos. On your own posts the app knows who liked it and shows that in the feed row but not in the sheet you opened to find out.

2Three anatomies

All three keep the app's laws: no hairlines, sentence case, one hue per surface, ink ground, the disc dial, no fake status. All three delete the spec table and replace its one honest fact with a sentence. They differ in what the sheet is a receipt of.
A · The post, then how it landed
The post renders at client fidelity — face, handle, words, media, quote — then one block answers "how did this land": counts as real numbers with the likers' faces beside them. Provenance is a sentence at the foot.

My recommendation: A, with C's parent card folded in. A is the only one of the three whose anatomy survives contact with the other ten shapes — a liked X post, a new follower and a Snapchat memory all have a "how it landed" and none of them has a thread. C is right about the parent (a card beats the label "Replying to @jessepollak", which tells you a name and withholds the sentence) but it makes the thread the frame, and eight of eleven shapes have no thread to be a node in. B is the honest loser: dotted leaders and a perforation are a claim that the app is joking, and the one place we already ruled receipt anatomy in (the approvals card, §292) took the anatomy and explicitly left the skeuomorph behind.

3One anatomy, the other shapes

A's proof: the same three blocks — who + words, how it landed, the dial — applied to the four shapes that draw nothing today. Nothing here needs a new Thing field, so no CloudKit Production deploy.

What "how it landed" is, precisely — so it can't drift into a tally. The module doctrine (§223) bans a count from being a thing; it does not ban a state surface from stating one, which is why the engagement line already exists. The block is a reading of one post, not news, and it renders only facts the record holds:
  • Live sources — the counts, read live on open (already built: SocialThread.engagement), plus the likers' names and faces on your own posts (already built: SocialLikers.roll, currently drawn only in the feed row).
  • Archives — the counts as the export recorded them, with the date said out loud. Never a live-looking number over a 2019 post.
  • Everything else — one sentence naming the provenance, which is what the From spec row was trying to say with a label column and an 80pt gutter.
  • An absent count has no cell. Unchanged, and the reason the block can be trusted at all.
