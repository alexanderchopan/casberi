Wallet feed — three directions

Same structure, same content, three stylings. All three apply the new label rule — a cell wears its symbol when one exists, its name only as the fallback (never both) — and all three shrink the holdings read from today's 220pt hero. Everything below the holdings (DeFi, transactions) is unchanged on purpose, so the variation reads cleanly.

A · The band
The treemap flattens into one proportional row, 72pt — a third of today's height. Width is the magnitude voice (same sqrt scale), cells stay the literal sheet tiles: top three, then the door. The header collapses label + subline into one quiet line. The composition becomes a spine you read past, not a hero you scroll through.
