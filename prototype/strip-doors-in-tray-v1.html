What this changes

  • The strip becomes one class of thing. Everything in it filters the feed. The extra gap that currently exists only to stop people reading the All circle as a door stops being necessary.
  • The whole row drags. Combined with the sticky-header fix already built, there is no fixed layer left to swallow a swipe.
  • The melt machinery can go. No fixed head means no fade mask and no per-chip opacity ramp — fadeClear, fadeRamp, stripInset, contentLead, headTrailingEdge all retire.
  • The cost, stated: Settings goes from always-visible to two taps (FAB → footer). That is the trade you named — rare destinations shouldn't hold permanent space. The avatar is currently the least reachable control in the app anyway, at the top of a phone.
  • Still to decide: whether All stays pinned in the tray header only, or also leads the strip. Shown here leading the strip and in the tray header — it is the way home from both places.
