Room gear

A gear on glass, floating top-trailing in a scoped room. One tap, straight to that source's setup screen — no long-press, no panel, no second state. It only ever appears when a room has a seat behind it, which is what makes a bare gear readable: it is never on screen at a moment when it could mean anything else.

42pt, floating, one tap. Glass on the composer/toast layer — the sanctioned use (§8: floating layer only, never on content). Nothing is pushed down; it sits over the first row's corner and the feed starts exactly where it starts today.

·

Presence is what makes a bare gear legible. It is drawn ONLY in a scoped room with a seat behind it — never in All, never in You or Voice or Pinboard. So at the moment you see it, there is exactly one thing it could be about, and it sits in the room's own body rather than up in the strip head beside the avatar and the catalogue, which are the genuinely app-wide controls.

·

Fades on scroll, returns when you stop — the trigger chrome.minimized already drives on the strip, so the two move together rather than fighting.

·

Outline, not filled. A filled glyph at this size next to a strip full of saturated brand marks reads as a sixth chip; the thin stroke reads as chrome, which is what it is.

The tap count is the smaller half. Settings is a rare act, so shaving four taps off it is a nicety. The real gain is the thing the catalogue route cannot do at all: tell you a seat is broken while you are standing in its room. Today the only signal is the room going quiet — which is §311's failure, and looks exactly like having nothing new.

What it costs

One view on the floating layer. No new stored field, no request, no CloudKit deploy — the seat resolves from BridgeCatalog and the alert state from BridgeApp.Status, both already computed for BridgeDetailScreen. It mounts on the shell beside the composer rather than on FeedScreen, so it survives room transitions instead of dying with the .id() subtree (§357). One thing worth guarding mechanically: the gear must resolve a folded label to its venue before it targets anything, since writing the label straight through is §351's central invariant and the failure is silent — a gear that opens the wrong seat's screen, or none.
