What changes, and why

  • “Room” leaves user-visible copy entirely. Two button strings and two notes. It's a PRD word; the user's model is the chip, so the page shows the chip rather than naming a place.
  • Connect ends in proof, on the page that made the promise. The header's existing connected: wash gets passed (it's built and unused on both screens), and the live proof line gives the page something true to say the moment you connect.
  • The comparison enters the catalog. Right now nothing in the catalog — tile, product page, or connect screen — mentions that the two venues can be compared. It becomes a check-list line on the product page and a direct ask on the connect screen, gated so it's absent once both are connected.
  • The twin ask becomes durable. Reachable from any followed market, any time, one request on demand — instead of a transient offer cleared by the next load.
  • Following rows say something. Live odds and the move since you followed, read free off PredictionPulse's cache — the same numbers MarketsAsk already reads, so they can never disagree with the feed.

Held back deliberately

  • No book on the catalog pages. One line, no list, no field, no chips — §234 stands. The line is the §235 lede, which already exists.
  • No auto-follow of the twin, and no “follow both” single tap — §234 refused both with reasons that still hold.
  • No payout, no stake, no “you'd have won.” The resolved receipt reports attention only.

Out of scope here, but worth knowing

  • With both exchanges connected, the disagreement cards render only in .all scope — and PredictionRoomBook defaults every room to its own venue. So the crown feature currently sits behind a segment tap. That's a room fix, not a catalog one; flagging it, not doing it.
