Wallet feed — three more directions

The treemap is settled (160pt, values, wash — it renders identically in all three frames below). These push the other parts, one each: A gives the balance the room's headline voice, B teaches the transaction stream to speak money, C teaches the wallet switcher what each wallet is worth. They're independent — any could ship alone or together.

A · The headline
The crown feature stops living in a tile. The combined balance becomes the room's display headline — set directly on the page, delta beside it, the sparkline running full-width underneath as a whisper. Worth a look steps down to one quiet line (it's usually empty; a permanent half-width card was reserving space for an exception). The reads start at the treemap; the page above it is pure voice.

B · The ledger stream
The reads stay exactly as they are — the stream learns to speak money. Every row states its USD effect in the trailing slot: receives in the confirm green (a real gain), sends in plain primary ink (spending isn't an alarm), a swap shows both marks and no signed number (it moved shape, not value). Day headers carry the day's honest net. The rows stop being generic thing rows and become a ledger you can actually read.

C · The switcher knows
Scoping stops being anonymous chips. Each wallet's switcher entry becomes a small account card — face, name, and what it's worth right now — so the question the switcher answers ("which wallet?") includes the fact you were switching to find. The selected card wears the tint; the balance tile then drops its number-repeating job when scoped and the pair reads like accounts in a bank app. Everything below is unchanged.
