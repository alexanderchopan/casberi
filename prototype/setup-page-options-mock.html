Setup page — three ways to make it short

All three are App Store Connect, not connected — the longest form in the catalogue, so whatever survives here fits everything else.

Fixed in all three, whichever wins: Notes and Who may read it do not exist until the seat is connected — they are facts about an account, and there is no account yet. Every line of copy is cut to fit one line at 13pt; nothing wraps, nothing orphans. State and mode share one line.
