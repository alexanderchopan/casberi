Wallet — the third shape

§351 folded Peer / Privacy Pools / Gnosis Pay / Railgun under the "Wallet" chip and left two things open: what the Wallet chip lands on, and how you move between the members without stacking three pinned bars. The proposal: the chip always lands on the balance room (Wallet has a home; Markets doesn't) · satellites are an in-content seat row there (doors scroll away; only scope controls pin — §136's own test) · member rooms get the Markets capsule with "Wallet" leading (the way home). The address bar only exists when source == "Wallet", the switcher only when it isn't — so nowhere renders more than two bars.

What this encodes

  • Wallet lands home, always. CategoryFold.landing gets a per-category anchor: Wallet resolves to the balance room, never last-visited. Markets keeps last-visited — no venue there is home.
  • Two bars, never three. The address bar gates on source == "Wallet" (shipped); the member switcher gates on source != "Wallet" within the fold. Mutually exclusive by the same test, one safeAreaInset — the §352 stack can't occur.
  • Doors scroll, scopes pin. The seat row is content (§136: pinning is earned by needing the control mid-stream). Facts come free from the §349 room heads — no new reads.
  • Attention is words, not rings. "1 needs proof" in attention orange on the seat itself; the dashed ring stays reserved for a connection that needs reconnecting.
