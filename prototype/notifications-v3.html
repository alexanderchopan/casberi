Notifications v3

One change from v2, and it’s yours: the right-hand slot now always carries something real — a photo when the thing holds one, and the source’s own mark when it doesn’t. Nothing is drawn by us.

Left — not a choice

iOS renders the sending app’s icon there on every notification. There is no API to change it, so the berry is fixed. The one exception is a “communication notification”, where a donated messaging intent replaces it with the sender’s face — Apple reserves that for real messaging apps, and claiming it for likes is an App Review risk. Not worth it. The repetition is also less of a problem than it looks: iOS stacks notifications by app, and the whole design keeps volume low — alarms plus one whisper.

Right — entirely ours

This is the slot you were reaching for, and it does the job you wanted: the left says who is telling you, the right says what it is about. A ladder, and it never invents: 1. a real photo the thing already holds — the liker’s avatar, a screenshot’s stored thumbnail. 2. failing that, the source’s own mark, the same asset the Apps catalog already ships. 3. if the source has no mark either, nothing — an honest blank, never a drawing.

What fires, and what it carries on the right

CLASS
FIRES ON
RIGHT-HAND SLOT
Alarm
A dispute opened · a new approval on a watched wallet · Privacy Pools cleared or asked for proof · a deadline inside 72h (evidence due, veAERO vote, lock expiry) · payments went silent
The source’s own mark — Stripe, the chain, 0xBow. Never a photo, because none of these sources serves one.
Arrival
Money in — an inbound transfer to a watched wallet, a Stripe payout paid. Attention in — likes, replies and new followers on your own posts, batched per post
Attention: the liker’s or replier’s real avatar. Money: the chain’s or Stripe’s mark.
Whisper
Once a day, at a time you pick. The line DayBrief already composes — nil when there is nothing to say, and then nothing fires at all
The newest screenshot’s own stored thumbnail when the day landed one; the busiest source’s mark otherwise.

Never fires: a landed row (a post, a link, a screenshot — the feed is the product and it waits), any count or balance reading (a state, not an event — §216), a trending token, a bridge finishing a sync, a charge succeeding. Batching: likes on one post collapse into one notification that updates in place as more arrive; a sweep finding eleven alarm-class events sends the worst and says “and 10 more”. Time-sensitive is claimed by the deadline alarms alone, so a 3am dispute can break through — everything else honors quiet hours. The ask comes the first time an alarm-class event actually exists, never at launch.
