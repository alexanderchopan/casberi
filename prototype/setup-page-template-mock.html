Disconnect

Notes is a box you can paste into — a labelled field on the page ground, three lines tall, growing to about eight before it scrolls. It replaces the one-line right-aligned field, which truncated anything longer than a phrase and read as a caption rather than a place to put something.

The template, as rules

  1. One page, two states. Header · act · facts · readers · notes · roster · exits — the same slots in the same order whether or not the seat is connected. Nothing appears or disappears on connect; the facts fill in.
  2. No slabs in the act. Every step is a plain row: disc · title · (fact + chevron) or (inline field). A row that opens something out of the app shows its host as the fact.
  3. One filled element on the page, and it is an input. Notes is a box because a note is pasted; a key stays a one-line secure entry showing dots — which is what it showed inside the box too.
  4. An entry row keeps its placeholder as its label, filling in with what you type. Half these fields are also the roster's filter (§639 amendment), and a search bar redrawn as "Search ⟩ value" is a settings row pretending to be a finder.
  5. The steps and the read boundary stay visible, quieted. Not folded into a row: the only two ways to do that are a sheet raised from inside a list row — the trap that broke the feed's first tap — or collapsing the permission boundary out of sight on the screen where you are about to paste a key. They drop to 13pt and inset to the title column instead.
  6. One verb, once, at the end of the act — plain tint text on the page ground, dim until the required fields are there. No blue CTA slab: the page's only loud element was the step you take first, which is the least important thing on it.
  7. Progress is shown by facts filling in, not by ticks: the file row shows the filename it read, the Key ID row shows the ten characters it derived.
