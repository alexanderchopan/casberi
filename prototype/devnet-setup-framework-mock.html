  • The field is at the top of the accounts card, above the examples: here is the box, and here are addresses to put in it if you have none of your own. It is one control (`DSSlabField`) on all four now, with the live address preview vibenet already had — three seats used a different field with a hand-rolled result line under it.
  • One row shape for every account — face, a claim in words, the address, a trailing verb. A row you have taken says ✓ Watching and stops being tappable; before this, three of the four either dropped the row or left it tappable doing nothing.
  • The two exits share one shape at the foot of the screen: a centred card row, the shape Disconnect already used. The explorer was a filled full-width slab with a chevron — the grammar of a door onto another screen in this app, which is not what it is.
  • Only the first watch routes. The 2026-08-28 vibenet ruling, generalised — a second watch never yanks the list away from the thumb still using it.
  • No key, no faucet here. §465: setup is once. Creating an account and topping up are the room's Home tiles on the three chains that have them; Hegotá's leftover "Get test ETH" door is deleted, as Frames' was on Sep 1.
  • The one place the four still differ is the Watching roster with Remove. Vibenet's lives on the room's face rail per §465; the other three rooms have no rail yet, so removing it there would leave a pasted address with no way to be unwatched.
