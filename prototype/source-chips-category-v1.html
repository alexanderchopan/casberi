The 2026-07-09 "labels made the row scroll" ruling was against per-source labels — dozens of them. A 44pt capsule holds every category name at full 13pt with air; the tint ring becomes a capsule stroke, the dashed attention ring survives unchanged, and the doors/"All"/pin keep their circles as the fixed anchors — words in capsules, marks in circles, one grammar each.

E · The iPad/Mac rail — the one surface no option above covered

The rail is a FIXED 88pt column, so a capsule there cannot size to its word. It sizes to the rail instead (68pt, uniform), which makes the type uniform for free: the container bounds the longest name, so nothing else has to. The landing mark sits the rail out — at 68pt a mark plus "Shopping" doesn't fit at a readable size, and a mark that appears for the short names and vanishes for the long ones is worse than none in a column whose whole grammar is uniformity. VoiceOver still speaks it, and the switcher is one tap away.
