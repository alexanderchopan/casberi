Reading them together.
  • A is the floor and C is the ceiling. C is A plus the one reading the unscoped room cannot otherwise give, and it degrades to A on its own when only one address is watched — so building A first costs nothing if C follows. B is the prettiest and the most fragile: it is the only one where each mark is a door, and the only one that dies on a busy address.
  • All three replace a reading that is not wrong, only redundant. Keep kindMix — the day a pool spend lands it is the better figure. The fix is that it should not be the ONLY one: when the mix is a single kind, draw the time figure instead, and the legend line becomes the caption under it.
  • The empty state's real bug is placement, not wording. empty() draws inside scopeList, below the rail, so the card above it is 300 blank points and the sentence is pushed under the fold. Whatever else is chosen, the empty case belongs in the slot — that alone fixes the screenshot.
  • D is the one that generalises. Frames, Spend keys, Snapshots and Sponsors all have the same nothing to say, and today each says it in its own sentence below the rail. One shape for all of them, naming the subject and offering the door, is fewer words and more answer.
  • On making every scope present: the cost is that four chips are empty for most people most of the time, and an empty chip that teaches is worth more than a hidden one — but only if the sentence is written per scope. Falling back to one shared "nothing here yet" for all four would be four chips saying the same thing, which is the strip §83 warns about wearing new clothes.
  • Independent of any of this: on the first screen head() reads quiet from spend keys and snapshots alone and never looks at moves — so "Nothing on this chain from the 2 addresses you watch" prints directly above three of their transfers. Worth fixing in the model before any drawing lands.
