Notes

  • The strip never moves. The faces sit inside the Wallet category, below the chips — so entering and leaving a wallet room changes only what's under the strip, never the strip's own position.
  • Selection is opacity and weight, not a ring. A ring already means "active chip" up in the strip and dashed orange means "needs reconnecting"; a third ring would collide. Unselected faces sit at 40%.
  • Faces are 36 in a 44pt slot. The slot is the tap target, so it clears the 44pt floor while the face stays subordinate to the room beneath it. A 56 shelf face is for in-content shelves, where the face is the content rather than the chrome.
  • The card has to name the scope — "vitalik.eth" in 2. Without it, a scoped Peer room and an unscoped one belonging mostly to one busy wallet render identically.
