Privacy Policy · Effective July 8, 2026

Your things stay yours.

Casberi is built so your data lives on your iPhone, not on our servers — because we don't run any. This page explains, plainly, what that means.

What Casberi stores, and where

Everything you capture or connect — notes, links, screenshots, events, chats, voice notes, and the things your connected apps bring in — is stored on your device, in Casberi's own local database. Casberi has no backend: your corpus is never uploaded to us, because there is no "us" server to upload it to.

iCloud sync (optional, off by default)

If you turn on iCloud sync, your things are mirrored through your own private iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit, so they can appear on your other devices. This is Apple's infrastructure, tied to your Apple ID — only you can read it, and Casberi never sees it. Sync is off until you choose to enable it, and turning it off (or using Delete things) removes the iCloud copy.

Connecting other apps

When you connect a service — a feed, a social handle, a token, a calendar — Casberi talks to that service directly from your iPhone to fetch your data. Nothing routes through a Casberi server.

  • For services that need a token or key, that credential is stored in your iPhone's Keychain and used only to call that service. It never leaves your device except to the service it belongs to.
  • For public data (a Bluesky handle, a Farcaster username, a public feed, a token's price), Casberi reads what's already public — no account of yours is required.
  • Each service you connect has its own privacy policy governing the data on their side; connecting it here doesn't change that.

On-device intelligence

On iPhones that support Apple Intelligence, Casberi's answers are written by Apple's on-device model, from your things, on your device. By default, your data is not sent to Casberi, to Apple's servers, or to any third party to generate them. The text inside your screenshots is read the same way — by Apple's Vision framework, on your device.

Your own AI key (optional, off by default)

If you add your own Anthropic API key in Settings, every answer gains a "Try with your key" button. Only when you tap it, your question and the few matched things are sent from your iPhone directly to Anthropic to write a stronger answer — never through a Casberi server, and never on their own. Answers written this way say so, right on the answer. The key lives in your iPhone's Keychain, goes only to Anthropic itself, and Anthropic bills you directly under Anthropic's privacy policy. Remove the key and the button disappears.

Price charts

When you watch a token, Casberi fetches its public price data from public market APIs (such as GeckoTerminal, Alchemy, and Dexscreener) to draw the chart. These requests carry only the token you're viewing — nothing about you or your identity.

What Casberi does not do

  • No analytics or usage tracking.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No selling, sharing, or renting of your data — there's nothing to sell, since we never receive it.
  • No accounts, no sign-up, no password to give us.

During the TestFlight beta

While Casberi is distributed through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may collect standard beta diagnostics — crash reports and basic usage metrics — under Apple's privacy policy, to help improve the app. This is Apple's collection, not Casberi's, and you can disable sharing in the TestFlight app.

Children

Casberi is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them. (It doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone — see above.)

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with an updated effective date.
