The name

Casberi is cache + berry — one container that holds many things.

Say it kaz-BEHR-ee — the raspberry stress pattern.

The front syllable carries cache (and a little casa — home); the tail carries the berry sound without the berry spelling. It's written like a name: one capital, no internal caps.

The mark

A berry built from seven opaque drupelets in one hue. Light enters from the top-left and fades across the cluster — many things, one container.

One hue, seven tones

Every drupelet is a solid pre-mixed step of #0A84FF toward the ground. No gradients, no alpha, no second hue.

Front occludes back

Drupelets draw dimmest-first so the bright front ones cover the back — the way a real berry sits.

Square canvas, no baked radius

The corners here are display-only. iOS applies the superellipse mask; ship the square art.

Don't redraw it

The files below are final art, legible down to 29px. No recoloring, restyling, mascots, or wordmark lockups.

Principles

Six rules govern every Casberi surface — the app, the site, and anything wearing the mark. None of them have exceptions.

Color earns its place

Color carries identity, state, or magnitude — never decoration. One tint does all the interactive work; a second hue must mean something.

Nothing draws a line

No hairlines, no dividers, no borders. Surfaces separate by spacing, tone, and a soft ambient shadow — zero exceptions.

Honest, or absent

No dead controls, no fake status, no placeholder numbers. A value is live or it isn't shown; a disabled control looks disabled.

Plain words

Copy speaks like a person — no sync, scope, token, or OAuth where a person reads it. Sentence case, always.

One animation per moment

Motion is 250 ms on Apple's sheet curve, and each moment gets one move. Delight is a single well-placed gesture, not a shimmer on everything.

Infrastructure, not a mascot

The agent has no face, no name, no persona. The berry is a container, not a character — it never gets eyes.
