Your history with what you watch.

Not a portfolio app. Not a market terminal. Watch any address — 0x…, an ENS name, a .sol, a basename — and what it does over time becomes yours to read.

Read-only and structural, not promised. Watching an address can never trade or move funds. WalletConnect proposes zero methods and zero events and tears the session down immediately. Every call is made straight from your iPhone — no server.

Getting in

  • Paste anything — an address, ENS, .sol, or a basename. .sol is tried first, so toly.sol resolves to a real wallet.
  • Connect a wallet app — hands over the address, read-only, never signs.
  • Peek at a public wallet — one tap to watch a famous address, then it retires forever.
  • From a Farcaster profile — “Watch their wallet” resolves the verified address.

The combined portfolio

  • Across your wallets — one total, digits rolling like an odometer between values, with a pill naming the window it covers.
  • A sparkline that draws itself, with transaction marks on the line — tap one and its sheet opens.
  • “Mostly ETH · +$310” — why it moved, not just that it did.
  • 7d / 30d / watched — offered only when the record reaches back that far.

What you hold

  • A treemap — top holdings as cards sized by value, staggering in. Tap a cell for that token’s live chart, drawn on-device.
  • “ETH is 62% of everything” — opens Where it’s held: every position, its share, and the wallets holding it.
  • Across-your-wallets sheet — a “Made of” band chart in each wallet’s own color, and “What moved” by token.
  • Wallet switcher — one chip per wallet; the selection slides between them, morphing into the destination’s color.

What lands in your feed

Every one of these arrives as an ordinary thing, searchable from Spotlight: received and sent transfers · self-transfers between your own wallets · swaps · Solana transfers and swaps · token approvals and revokes · NFT operator grants · Peer fills (“Bought 25 USDC with Venmo”) · delegation changes · Aave liquidation-risk alerts · Safe pending signatures.

  • Transaction sheets are depicted, not described — two faces with the amount flowing between them; both legs of a swap, both your own marks for a move.
  • Counterparties get names — your own label, then a watched Farcaster handle, then another watched wallet, then known contracts, then reverse ENS. The title never carries a raw hash.

Worth a look

A quiet line that whispers when it’s clear and speaks up when it isn’t — “1 delegation · 1 flagged transfer.”

  • Token approvals — new grants land as things; the row opens that wallet’s Revoke.cash page.
  • Address poisoning — an incoming transfer mimicking an address you’ve sent to gets flagged: “Looks like a copy of an address you’ve used.” It never hides the transfer.
  • EIP-7702 delegation — your wallet starting to delegate its control lands as a thing.
  • Aave health factor — collateral, debt, health, and an alert on a new crossing below 1.5.
  • Safe multisig — “2 of 3 signatures collected.”
  • The approval prepare card — re-reads the grant’s live state, quotes the revoke fee, and hands you copyable transaction JSON. A transaction you sign there — never in Casberi.

Ask it, in plain language

All computed live from your own things. No model, no guessing.

  • “How’s my wallet” — a full brief: sparkline, approvals, activity, tokens, treemap, allocation, newest things.
  • “How’s my watchlist”
  • “How’s my Aave loan” · “health factor”
  • “What have I spent on gas”
  • “Anything pending on my Safe”
  • And the away recap: “Your wallets are up 3.1% while you were away.”

A wallet is a color

  • One seed drives a wallet’s identicon and its tint — and that hue carries through the switcher chip, the sheet’s bands, the allocation tray, and every held row.
  • ENS avatars replace identicons when published.
  • A new high rains berries in the wallet’s own hue — “Across your wallets: a new high, $19.9K.” Baselines seed silently; drawdowns never fire.

Chains: Ethereum · Base · Arbitrum · Optimism · Polygon · Solana · Robinhood Chain — and each address is routed only to the chains its shape can live on.
