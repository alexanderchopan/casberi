FAQ

Questions.

Is it free?

Yes. There's no subscription and no account. Answers run on your own phone, so there's nothing to bill.

What phones does it work on?

iPhone, on iOS 26. Answers written by AI need Apple Intelligence; on other iPhones, search works the same way without it.

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone. If you turn on iCloud sync, it also lives in your own iCloud — under your Apple account, not ours.

Can I get my data out?

Yes. Export saves everything to one file. Delete clears everything from your phone and from iCloud, with no undo.

Does typing in the search bar save things?

No. Things are only saved when you capture them — by sharing, speaking, pasting, or connecting an app. Search is just search.

When can I get it?

It's in development, headed to TestFlight and then the App Store. Leave your email and you'll be first to know.
