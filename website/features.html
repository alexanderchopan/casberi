Everything Casberi does

Every feature, one line each. Tap any of them for how it works.

Just connect

Own your dataNo account, no sign-in. It’s all on your phone. Connect your appsMail, photos, wallets, calendars, and dozens more. Sign in with GitHubA short code, one tap to github.com, and the connection makes itself — no token hunting. Read-only, revocable anytime. Screenshots, readableThe text in your screenshots is read on your iPhone — search it, ask about it. Watch one feedOne screen, no tabs. Everything together, or one app at a time. DiscoverFresh finds land on their own — trending tokens and just-launched NFT drops, right in your feed. One screenChips up top, one button below. That’s the app.

Keep tabs

Pin your favoritesBuild a board, arranged your way. Coming upYour board leads with what’s next — upcoming events and due reminders, soonest first. While you were awayCome back to one chip that sums up what landed while you were gone. Act on itemsFrom your feed: open, pin, or share, in the app it came from. Tag your thingsGroup without folders. One thing, many tags. Apps take shapeCalendar reads like an agenda, Photos becomes a grid, a wallet leads with its balance. Across your walletsWatch a few wallets and see them as one — a combined view over everything you hold. Watch a priceTrack a product or a token — it lands back in your feed when the price moves.

Take action

Ask anythingA question, answered from your own things — on your phone. Organize everythingTell it to tag or rename across the lot. It shows the change before Apply. Try with your keyAdd your own key and any answer can re-run on a bigger model — Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, or Venice. Only when you tap, straight from your iPhone. Finish at the sourceComplete a Todoist task or close a GitHub issue right from the thing. It asks first, every time. Ask from anywhereSearch Casberi and Ask Casberi ride Shortcuts and Siri — pipe your things into any automation. Jump to toolsNote, message, email, event, reminder, search, ChatGPT, or Claude. Capture fastPaste something to save it, or tap the mic and talk.

Make it yours

AvatarYour photo, up top. BackgroundA color or a picture of yours, behind your board. LanguageEnglish, Español, 中文, 日本語, 한국어. DataLives on your phone, syncs only if you turn it on. Export, import — and two clear wipes: your things, or Casberi’s access.
