The mark — a berry built from seven opaque drupelets in one hue.
Light enters from the top-left and fades across the cluster.

Rules
  - The art is final. Do not re-draw, recolor, add gradients, or bake a
    corner radius — iOS applies the superellipse mask.
  - One hue only: #0A84FF. Magnitude renders as tone steps of that hue.
  - Square canvas, no baked radius. Legible at 60px and 29px.

Name
  Casberi — kaz-BEHR-ee. Cache + berry. Written like a name: Casberi,
