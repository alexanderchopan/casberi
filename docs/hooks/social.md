# Social networks — Farcaster, Bluesky, Nostr, Telegram

Moved out of `CLAUDE.md` on 2026-09-05 so the always-loaded file stays a rule sheet.
Every entry below is **verbatim** as it was written — nothing was summarised, shortened or dropped.
`CLAUDE.md` carries a one-line index pointing here, keyed by the hook flag or the entry title.

- **The social rooms became ONE room (`Model/SocialRoom.swift` + `SocialRoomSource.swift`, `scripts/social-room-selftest.sh`, 2026-08-26, prd §489).** Nine sources land the same three things — a post with an author, a shared link, a conversation — and the `Thing` model is identical for all of them; what differed was the ROOM, decided by `FeedScreen.Shape.init`, a switch each source joined by copying the last with the row rules re-spelled inline every time. **Nostr had NO case at all**, so its rows fell to the generic band while `PostCard.author`, `SocialThreadCard`, `SocialThread.replies` and `NostrStore.socialAccounts` all already carried Nostr code nothing could reach — the reported symptom ("nostr doesn't even have avatars showing at the top") was the face rail, gated on the same switch. **TikTok had none either** (saved videos as 80-character title rows, found by auditing the other seven, not by a report), and **§396a's "a post card never merges" was repaired for X and never carried to Instagram or Telegram**, both of which drew post cards squeezed into a merged run. **`rowKind` is the one answer now and `standsAlone` is DERIVED from it rather than spelled beside it** — which is what makes the §396a class structurally impossible rather than fixed per room; `isXPostRow`, `isTelegramPostRow`, Instagram's inline chain and the roster ternary are deleted, and `FeedScreen`'s five row branches are one `socialRow` with no rules of its own. **THE ROSTER TERNARY IS THE TRAP TO KNOW**: `rosterAccounts` was `source == "Farcaster" ? FarcasterStore : BlueskyStore`, so adding `case "Nostr"` to `Shape.init` alone turns the rail on and fills it with BLUESKY's watched accounts — a room that looks fixed and answers with the wrong person's posts; `SocialRoomSource.accounts(for:)` is the one dispatch, read by the room and by `MainSurface` both. Grid membership deliberately STAYS in the view layer (it reads `previewImageData`, it is layout, and the four mixed rooms answer differently for stated reasons) and is guarded both ways. Two deliberate changes to shipped rooms: a day says "posts" only when every row in the group is one (X's §396a rule generalised — twelve casts and one shared article was calling the article a thirteenth post), and a shared article or a follow notification no longer takes a card of its own. **The last block of the harness is the part that holds**: every catalog `Network` seat must resolve in `SocialRoom.table` or be named in `KNOWN_NO_ROOM` with a reason (empty today) — `social-sheet-selftest.sh`'s assertion for the room, and the reason the SHEET was the one surface that had not drifted. Snapchat is IN the table answering `drawsPosts` false, so the guard sees it decided rather than forgotten. Its negative guards read a COMMENT-STRIPPED copy (all three files document the dialects they replaced by naming them — the Obsidian/Cursor lesson, eighth instance). **Until this pass NO check in this repo could reach these rules**: they lived inside an 8,900-line SwiftUI view, so no `swiftc` harness could compile them, the screen sweep proves a room painted and never that it painted the right anatomy, and the liveness audit asks about tombstones. **UNSEEN on a device** — both platforms compile and the harness is green, but no screenshot of any of these rooms was taken; a Nostr room with two watched accounts is the one to look at first, since its rail, its rings and its person filter all turn on together.

- Social grew an INBOUND half (2026-07-31, prd §239) — every read before it was outbound (what they post / liked / recast / who they follow), the one exception being mentions. Three additions, mirrored across Farcaster and Bluesky. (1) **Amplification**: a `recasts` toggle per account — `-fcRecasts <username>` (`reactionsByFid` one reaction type over, so `landLikes` became `landReactions(type:why:)`), `-bskyReposts <handle>` (costs NO extra request — reposts already ride the author feed as entries wearing a `reason`, and `land`'s `ownHandle` filter was silently dropping every one; Bluesky also gained §221's resurface, which was Farcaster-only). One marker (`socialContext == "recast"`), two nouns — "Recast" vs "Reposted" via `SocialThread.recastWord`. (2) **`mine`** — the flag §221 said the stores lacked, turning on three reads in `Model/SocialInbound.swift`: replies received (the gap neither network could see — a reply names its parent in the RECORD, not the text, so `castsByMention`/`searchPosts mentions:` structurally cannot find one), likes received (§221 from your post's side, not the liker's; **names, not numbers** — the count fills `likeCount` and never lands as a record), and new followers (first sight seeds SILENTLY, the Peer/Morpho/Hyperliquid rule). Probes `-fcMine`/`-bskyMine`, and `-inboundProbe YES` for ledger sizes + eligible-post counts (a landed count alone can't separate "nobody replied" from "the read never ran", and a silent first pass is CORRECT). **Bounded**: both networks need one request per post for each post read, so a pass is `2 × ownPostPage` (6) per account marked yours; followers cap at 5/pass. **`ownPostWindow` (7d) is a PREFERENCE, not a gate, since 2026-08-07 (prd §331)** — as a hard cutoff it switched the whole inbound half OFF for anyone who posts less than weekly (newest cast 8 days old → likes and replies read nothing, forever, with no error and nothing on screen to say why; reported as "i marked a farcaster profile as mine and expected to see likes given to me, i even tapped that"). When the window holds nothing the read falls back to your newest posts regardless of age; `ownPostPage` bounds it either way, so the cost is unchanged and a daily poster keeps exactly the old behaviour. `-inboundProbe` names which road the posts came by. The footer copy was rewritten with it — it read "likes bring your posts back", which described the §221 resurface alone (watched likers only, so almost never) and never said you'd see WHO. **The follower ledger is a SET, not a cursor** — forced, not chosen: Bluesky's `getFollowers` carries NO follow timestamp anywhere in its payload. Capped at 500, forgotten on disconnect and on flipping `mine` off. All three Bluesky reads are on the UNAUTHENTICATED AppView host — what happens TO you is public there even though what you did (`getActorLikes`) is not. (3) **Signers** (`Model/FarcasterSigners.swift`, `-fcSignerProbe <username|YES>`) — which apps can post as you, read off the Optimism Key Registry via `onChainEventsByFid` (asked BEFORE `onChainSignersByFid` because only the filtered form can carry REMOVES). `WalletApprovals` for social identity, inheriting prd §112's preparing-surface ruling: reads and previews, links to Farcaster's connected-apps settings the way an approval links to Revoke.cash. First sight lands the current INVENTORY rather than seeding silently — a deliberate divergence, justified by the events carrying their own block timestamps, so it sorts years back where it can't fake urgency; the news-only MOMENT rule still holds. An app is NAMED only when the metadata decodes to a plausible fid AND that fid resolves — a wrong name on a permission notice sends you to revoke the wrong thing. **UNMEASURED** (authored with no Xcode and no egress): check whether the AppView returns reposts under `filter=posts_no_replies`, the signer `events` envelope + `blockTimestamp` units, and the `SignedKeyRequestMetadata` ABI offset. Each fails SAFE.

- Farcaster grew likes/mentions/channels/replies (2026-07-14, same keyless Snapchain node): `-fcName <username[,username]>` — watch accounts and sync; `-fcLikes <username>` — watch an account's LIKES (adds the account if new; liked casts land as things, stamped with the like's time); `-fcMentions <username>` — watch MENTIONS of an account (casts naming them land, riding "while I was away"); `-fcChannel <name[,name]>` — follow channels (name → parent URL via the api.farcaster.xyz channel directory, cached like an fid); `-fcReplies "<username>:<0xhash>"` — fetch a cast's thread and NSLog count + first line (the thing sheet's Replies section, headless). Fire Farcaster probes ONE per launch — the refresh's running guard makes a concurrent one report 0.

- Social enrichment (2026-07-16, prd 81) — both networks, one pass through `Model/SocialBridge.swift`. New `Thing` fields (all additive optionals, no migration): `postText` (the FULL post — `title` is still the 80-char clamp and `content` is still the PERMALINK, so open/share/route logic is untouched), `socialContext` ("liked"/"mention") + `channelName` (WHY it's here — the row's trailing slot says "Liked"/"/design"/"Mentions you", beating the handle there), `likeCount`/`repostCount`/`replyCount`, `quote`/`parent` (`SocialCard` — face, words, permalink, and the PROTOCOL `ref`), `imageURLs` (all of them). Probes: `-socialProbe <Bluesky|Farcaster>` reports what actually landed across the corpus (full text / images / quotes / parents / markers / walkable quotes) — the fastest check that a social change works; `-bskyFeed <query|at-uri>` follows a Bluesky FEED (its channel analog — at-uri, found by SEARCH since Bluesky has no global channel names; prd 75's held question). Deep link `casberi://person/<Source>/<handle>` opens the profile card headlessly. Two lessons paid for: (1) a post already in the corpus dedupes OUT of the landing path, so every ingest heals on the dedupe hit — without that, enrichment reaches only posts landed from that day on; (2) a Farcaster web permalink carries only the FIRST 10 CHARS of the cast hash, so it can never stand in for the protocol ref when reading a thread — that's what `SocialCard.ref` is for.

- **Who liked your post (2026-08-07, prd §330, `Model/SocialLikers.swift`, `-likersProbe YES`).** §239's inbound half stated "names, not numbers" and shipped only the numbers: `readLikes` on BOTH networks filled `likeCount` and threw every liker's identity away unless the liker was already an account you WATCH — and even then the only output was a `SourceMoments` toast that scrolls away. So "who liked my post?" had no answer on a row, in a sheet, or in the corpus. **Farcaster had it worse for a structural reason**: Bluesky's AppView hydrates `like.actor.handle` inline (free), Snapchain reports **fids only** (one `userDataByFid` per name), so the §306 pass wired `Notifications.likes` — written to be "called from the read, because the NAMES exist only inside the read itself" — for Bluesky and never for Farcaster. Now both reads keep the names: Farcaster resolves the **newest three** through the per-launch profile cache and no more (three is what a line shows anyway, and it bounds the added cost at `3 × ownPostPage` lookups), ordered by the **timestamps on the wire** rather than the page's order (this call passes no `reverse`, and which liker leads is exactly what an assumed order gets quietly wrong); the rest are counted, never named; your own like is dropped from the NAMES while `likeCount` keeps counting it; and a full page reads "and 97+ others" (`SocialCount`'s honesty valve, spoken out loud). **A roll with no resolvable name is never written** — a total with nobody named is the tally the doctrine forbids, and it's what the sheet's engagement line already shows. Kept in UserDefaults (the `ASCState`/`X402State` shape), capped at 60 posts, dropped on disconnect — **NOT a `Thing` field**: a like is a PROPERTY of your post, and a new stored property is a CloudKit Production deploy for a fact re-read every foreground pass. Drawn by `PostCard`, under the post; no "is this mine?" test is needed or made, since the store is written by the inbound read alone and that read only ever asks about your own casts. `-likersProbe` prints the whole book across BOTH networks (one `liker|` per post — names, total, floor-or-not, and the line the row draws) because an empty book has FIVE causes that render identically — no account marked `mine`, a first sync whose own posts hadn't landed so the read never ran, nobody liked anything inside `ownPostWindow`, the only liker was you, or the node answered `reactionsByCast` and then refused every `userDataByFid` — and only the last is a bug; a book holding one network's rolls and not the other's is the shape of the bug this fixed. **UNBUILT/UNMEASURED** (Linux, no Xcode, no egress to either node); every static gate passes, `verify.sh` has not run.

- Follow import (2026-07-16, prd 87) — "Who they follow": both networks' follow graphs are keyless and HYDRATED (face/name/handle inline), read in `Model/SocialFollows.swift`, landed by a picker (`Screens/FollowImportSheet.swift`) reached from a capsule on the watched-account row beside "Watch their wallet". It's a picker BY RULING, not a mirror — a measured account follows 1,848 people (another 3,757), so auto-following is a timeline plus a sync job per person; nothing is preselected and the list stays in network order (no rank we didn't compute). `-followsProbe "<Bluesky|Farcaster>:<handle>"` reads a graph and NSLogs count + `truncated` + hydration + the first three rows (it watches NOBODY — the taps that watch are the person's). **Measured quirks, don't "fix" without re-measuring:** (1) Farcaster's client API allows **20 requests per 10 seconds** and `SocialFollows.pageDelay` (500ms) is load bearing — unpaced, a 1,848-follow graph returned exactly **999 people wearing "that's all"** (`IngestSupport.run` maps non-200 → nil, so the walk silently stops and hands back a prefix); (2) that ceiling is **per connection**, so it does NOT reproduce with curl/urllib (fresh connection per request — those walked all 37 pages clean and said the endpoint was fine, three times, while the app died at page 21 every run) — reproduce against ONE keep-alive connection like URLSession uses, or you'll delete the delay; (3) Bluesky needs no pacing (40 back-to-back pages, no limit) — the two arms differ on purpose; (4) `limit` is schema-capped at **50** on Farcaster (asking 100 is a 400, not a clamp) and 100 on Bluesky; (5) Snapchain answers the graph too (`linksByFid`) but only as target FIDs with no batch fid→username — 1,848 lookups vs 37 requests; it is NOT pruning (an early theory, checked and false: it agreed with the client API on dwr to within the self-follow it counts).

- Bluesky mirrored the keyless parity set (2026-07-14, AT Protocol AppView): profiles + replies + mentions, sharing ONE renderer with Farcaster via `Model/SocialBridge.swift` (`SocialAccount`/`SocialWatch` for the setup rows, `SocialReply`/`SocialThread.replies(for:)` for the sheet). `-bskyMentions <handle>` — watch MENTIONS of a Bluesky account (searchPosts `mentions:handle`; adds the account if new); `-bskyReplies "at://…"` — fetch a post's thread by its at-uri (a Bluesky thing's sourceRef is `bsky:<at-uri>`) and NSLog count + first line. Host quirk: everything rides `public.api.bsky.app` EXCEPT searchPosts, which 403s there and is served keyless on `api.bsky.app` (the host the official client uses) — the mentions call, and only it, uses that host. Bluesky LIKES stay unbuilt: getActorLikes is auth-only (needs an app-password sign-in), and the catalog copy already says "Likes arrive with sign-in, later." Bluesky channels were deliberately held (its analog is custom feeds/lists by at-uri, a discovery-search UX, not follow-by-name).
