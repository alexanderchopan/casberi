# PRD in one table

A synthesis of docs/prd.md §3–§12. It does not replace the PRD — specs, rulings, and evidence stay there. Each row is one goal (or operate cost); each cell carries both rungs: **B** = Bob (app scale), **A** = Alice (machine-and-agent scale).

| User goal | How it works today | Outcomes (evidence) | Problem | Solution |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Stay mobile** — the phone completes the job | B: one app at a time; tasks cross apps in series, content moves by copy → switch → paste. A: the phone is a remote — ChatGPT on it, the actual labor on laptop/server; checking work means SSH and a terminal at 390pt wide | B: dinner planned in ChatGPT — date copied to Calendar, guests texted, booking in Safari, guest list stranded in the chat (Bob 5). A: on the train she can prompt a chat and check nothing (Alice 8) | **P1. Mobile gets hard.** A five-step job costs four boundary crossings (B); the phone can't reach or act on her machines (A) | **S4. Captures become outcomes.** The thing carries its next action. B: writes exit through bridges; the connected app finishes the job. A: output lands in Feed; the follow-up job leaves from the sheet — the phone directs what the machines run. (Killed S7's intent lives here.) |
| **2. Capture things** — any thought, image, link, in the moment | B: screenshots, Notes, Siri reminders, Messages-to-self, camera — each surface writes to its own container, and the moment's choice is permanent. A: a job's output lands wherever the run happened — repo, log, or chat | B: recipe in Notes, untitled — never found, never cooked (Bob 1); workout plan screenshotted, gym gets week one only (Bob 3). A: report in the repo, summary in the chat, log in the gateway — the client gets the version missing her correction (Alice 6) | **P2. Capture betrays them.** The container is picked by the moment (B) or by the machinery (A), not by the person | **S1. One home** — everything lands as things; a job is ONE thing (prompt, output, approval, writeup). **S3. Capture in one gesture** — share sheet, screenshot ingest, Siri, paste chip, mic; no destination, title, or folder step. A: her machines capture for her — connect once and output flows in |
| **3. Organize things** — order among the things | B: folders, lists, albums per app; the agent adds its own containers (threads, Projects, Library, Memory). A: one job scatters across four records — prompt in the terminal, output in a repo, approval in the gateway, writeup in a chat | B: second agent means a second history, memory, and settings panel — deleted in a week (Bob 8). A: "which agent broke the build" takes forty minutes across two session logs and a chat (Alice 3) | **P3. They can't organize.** Order costs labor per container and nothing spans containers; no unit of "job" exists anywhere | **S1** again, plus computed order instead of filing: projects are computed clusters (rename or pin, never file), type tags at ingestion, user tags on the sheet, marks by swipe or suggestion. No tag-management screen |
| **4. Find things** — retrieve by what a thing is | B: per-app search, Spotlight, chat scroll — retrieval starts with "which app." A: grep, scrollback, dashboards — retrieval starts with "which world, which machine, which session"; provenance lives nowhere | B: itinerary 40 messages deep in one of three chats; at the airport he re-asks and the new version drops the hotel address (Bob 2, also 1). A: reconstruction is her labor (Alice 3) | **P4. They can't find.** No search spans phone plus agent (B) or worlds and machines (A); failure lands at the moment of use, and he re-makes what he made | **S2. Find by what it is.** One search over the home: query by content, get the thing, not the thread. A: provenance as fields — source, agent, run, machine; "which agent broke the build" is a query. Shipped as the composer Ask path: on-device retrieval + Foundation Models compose over the retrieved things only (honesty rails: real things, never invented) |
| **5. Do things** — finish: plan, book, draft, schedule | B: the agent drafts but writes nothing to Calendar, Reminders, or Notes; output leaves the chat only by hand, and those trips stall. A: writes block on her judgment; the approval push arrives without the context, which stays where the work is | B: dentist reminder snoozed six times because booking takes a call (Bob 4); guest list never leaves the chat (Bob 5). A: the diff exceeds the notification, she approves on trust, the write breaks a config (Alice 2) | **P5. They can't finish.** Output is stranded one copy-paste away from done (B); approve-on-trust or stall the pipeline (A) | **S4** (verbs on the thing: App Intents and bridge writes, gated by ask-before-acting). **S9. Apps are bridges** — framework / account / import grades, each stated truthfully. **S10. Approvals carry context** — diff, scope, agent, target rendered for the phone; approve or block from the sheet (dormant for Bob). Write model: rungs 1–2 shipped, rung 3 per proven bridge, rung 4 behind approvals |
| **6. Own things** — privacy as the default | B: his record is split across Apple's apps and a chat vendor's servers. A: split further across vendors, gateways, and machines; none of it portable | No numbered evidence row — the PRD treats ownership as a standing requirement rather than an observed failure | Not a numbered problem; it is the constraint that decides the platform | Platform (§14): native iOS — SwiftData on device, CloudKit sync, no server holds things, reads and capture work offline, telemetry default off. Data sheet makes it operable: live counts, Export/Import, Delete everything (including sidecars). Answers run on the person's own device |
| **Operate cost: configure** (not a goal — spend against the six) | B: the agent's power sits behind unexplained settings in builder vocabulary; onboarding never walks him there. A: she onboards herself — keys, scopes, configs paid per machine, per agent, per update | B: opened ChatGPT Settings once, connected nothing, closed it (Bob 6). A: rebuilt the March invoice-parsing skill in June (Alice 4); new laptop cost two days, and one wrong scope got blamed on the model for a month (Alice 7) | **P6. They won't configure — or the spend evaporates.** Same problem at both rungs: operate spend against a product that banks none of it | **S5. Setup accrues.** B: zero setup — useful before it asks, permission requests arrive at the moment they unlock something. A: pay once, keep it — keys, scopes, skills saved as things (the thing is the address; the secret lives in Keychain); a new machine restores from the corpus. **S8. Apple-grade feel** is how the promise lands — felt, not announced |
| **Operate cost: agent count** | B: one agent app. A: five — each with its own account, history, memory, and settings, sharing nothing | B: downloaded a second agent, got two of everything, deleted it in a week (Bob 8). A: her stack pays that cost five times over — each addition re-levies P3, P4, P6 | **P7. They won't add another agent — or pay for five.** Agents share nothing; every addition multiplies containers | **S6. Not another agent.** Presents as an organizer: no model picker, no memory panel, no persona. Agents are rows in Apps and labor in Feed — the sixth agent adds a row, not a world |
| **Stay aware** (the push side of goals 4–5) | B: what he keeps stays mute — the corpus waits for a query he won't think to ask. A: machines finish, fail, and die silently; she learns by polling or by absence, and a missed approval push is gone | B: book rec sits in a screenshot while he stands in the bookstore (Bob 7); workout screenshot never advances (Bob 3). A: a run's result sits nine hours overnight (Alice 1); a dead cron job goes unnoticed for a week (Alice 5) | **P8. They aren't aware.** The state exists and never arrives; the monitoring tax is P8 paid by hand | **S11. State lands as things.** Run finished, run failed, cron died — each a Feed row; an expected thing that fails to arrive is itself a thing. Bob's rung parked: surfacing the right saved thing at the right moment needs context signals the build doesn't have yet |

**Held risks (§12):** the same primitives must absorb Alice's magnitude — Feed load, approval latency, thing granularity. Reversal trigger: if approvals pile past what Feed carries, a queue earns its own screen. Bob proves the primitives; Alice stress-tests them; build order stays Bob-first.
