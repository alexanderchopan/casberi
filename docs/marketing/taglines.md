## Decisions behind them

- **Hierarchy:** what it is → why it's safe → why it's free. Lead with the job (one place / keeps tabs), let *private* land as the enabler, treat *open* (any model) as the third beat — not the headline.
- **"On what matters" stays** — it's the word that makes it a smart feed, not a firehose. The agent's job is relevance, not conversation.
- **"All on device" beats "use any model"** when there's only room for one. It's the reassurance that makes watching all your apps feel safe — it answers the fear the first sentence creates. "Use any model" is the *second* tweet, not the scarce slot (and BYOK is becoming table stakes; fully-local aggregation is the rare, hard-to-copy claim).
- **Private is the enabler, not a parallel selling point.** A feed this personal — watching your logged-in streams — is only possible *because* it's local. Same argument, not two.
- **Don't lead with "bring your own key"** — it reads as a dev tool. It belongs last, as sovereignty.
- **Positioning:** a tool/companion that keeps tabs — *not* a home, not a replacement for your apps. Stays true while the product grows.
- **Banned word:** "corpus."
